Akrogon · operator guide

Two agents build it. You decide what.

Akrogon runs software work through two AI agents from different vendors. They plan, build, review, and merge on their own. You write down what you want, answer questions, and read the result. This guide explains every moving part in plain words, then walks through a normal working day. Read the pages in order. Each one ends with a link to the next.
