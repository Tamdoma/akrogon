# Brief: budget-rules

## What

Rewrite the paid-run rules so seats and the chart door budget paid test sessions themselves under the `paid_budget` ceiling and the `akrogon run-check --sessions` reservation that session-ceiling lands (this leaf consumes that flag, the key and the `AKROGON_SESSIONS`/`AKROGON_SESSION_MINUTES` variables). Repeat counts, renewals, count lines and the warn-past-bound rule leave the rules; the fresh-call trigger and the sufficiency floor move into `skills/lifecycle-rules.md`; standing grants (`approved.by: standing`) replace per-leaf operator approval for paid launches on logged-in accounts; the paid e2e entry points refuse to start unreserved. Outcome: no lifecycle or door rule asks the operator for a paid session count, and no paid test session runs outside the ceiling.

## Why

The operator approved four separate session counts for one leaf and a review asked for a fifth after a repair (watch-consults, 2026-11-21/22). The rules that cause those stops are prose spread over the lifecycle skills, the door and docs; replacing them removes the stops while the machine ceiling keeps the budget (signposts S1-S6).
