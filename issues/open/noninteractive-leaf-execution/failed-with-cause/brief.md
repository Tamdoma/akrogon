# Brief: failed-with-cause

## What
`failed` becomes a first-class stop. `akrogon phase <slug> failed --reason <text> [--slot <A|B>]` is legal from every active phase (plan.positions, plan.rebuttal, plan.synthesis, implement, check.review, check.fix, merge); `--slot` is required exactly when the phase requires two seats. State records `failure: { cause: 'blocked' | 'attempts', phase: <interrupted phase>, slot: <A|B>, reason: <text> }`. The move into `failed` is an immediate stop: it skips the clean-worktree, issue-diff and non-empty checks, the rebuttal destination check, the verdict requirement, the two-seat completion barrier and the review destination override, validates only that the declaring seat is required and unfinished, and never touches the worktree. The move out of `failed` to any active phase, `check.fix` included, skips the clean-worktree check when `failure.cause` is `blocked`, and removes `failure` so the new attempt starts clean. Moves into `failed` and `merged` clear `busy_since`, `busy_notified`, `prompted` and `prompted_at`; `tab`, `worktree` and `pane` stay. `next` skips seat observation for `failed` leaves exactly as it does for `merged`, so a still-blocked pane cannot recreate busy fields. `activeCount` ignores leaves in `failed` in both its branches, including the unreadable-inventory branch. `akrogon status` prints `failed <cause> <reason>` for a failed leaf, `failed` alone for a legacy record without `failure`, and no busy age for failed or merged leaves. Both existing failure producers record `cause: 'attempts'`: the dispatch attempts cap (reason `attempts exhausted`) and review repair exhaustion in `transition` (reason `fix rounds exhausted`).

## Why
Last night a seat that could not proceed had no legal way to stop, the board showed `busy` for 7 hours, and merged leaves still showed a busy age (Tamdoma/akrogon#19, #20 items 3 and 6). Recovery today is blocked by the clean check on preserved work, and stopped attempts hold seats.

## Done-criteria
1. `bun test` passes with new tests: `phase <slug> failed --reason x` from `implement` on a dirty worktree succeeds, records `failure.cause = blocked`, `failure.phase = implement`, keeps the dirty file, and clears busy fields.
2. Stops from `plan.positions` with rebuttal on and off, from a two-seat `check.review` without a verdict and with the peer unfinished, and from `merge` each land in `failed` immediately with no phase advance; a stop naming a slot that is not required or already done is refused.
3. `phase <slug> implement` from a `cause: blocked` failure succeeds with the worktree still dirty and removes `failure`; the same move from a `cause: attempts` failure still refuses a dirty worktree.
4. `failed -> check.fix` is legal; `failed -> merged` is not; `phase <slug> failed` without `--reason`, and with `--reason` on any other destination, is refused.
5. A test moving a leaf to `merged` shows empty `busy_since`; repeated `next` runs against a failed leaf whose pane still reports `blocked` leave busy fields empty and send no prompt.
6. `max_active` reached only by failed leaves still dispatches a ready leaf, in both the readable and the unreadable-inventory branch.
7. `akrogon status` prints the cause and reason for a failed leaf and `failed` alone for a legacy failed record without `failure`; review repair exhaustion records `cause: attempts`.
