# Brief: direct-leaf

## What

A chart-issues direct-route attempt becomes a real leaf with one new optional state field, `direct: true`. The door (slot A) builds it in its own terminal and the named reviewer (slot B) reviews it, as today, but the record, the phase moves, the landing and the broadcast are the command's:

1. `akrogon direct <slug> --reviewer <address> [--source <owner/repo#n>]...`, run by the door inside hc after it writes the leaf's ISSUE.md, brief.md, design.md and readiness.yaml, creates the unlocked worktree and `state.yaml` (`phase: implement`, `sources`, `frozen: {}`, `seat.A` = the caller, `seat.B` = the reviewer). Rerun on an existing direct leaf in any phase, it rewrites the two seat refs only.
2. `akrogon direct-drop <slug> --chart <chart folder>` ends an unmerged direct attempt outside `merge`: it stops live runs, removes the worktree and branch, and moves the owner folder into `<chart folder>/direct/`.
3. The command never starts, prompts, ends, kills, sweeps, renames or closes the door or reviewer terminals, never records a `tab`, and never counts a direct leaf toward `max_active`.
4. `akrogon status` shows a direct leaf like a lifecycle leaf: phase, NOTE (busy, verdict, fix rounds, land, broadcast pending), TIME from the attempt's creation, and the recorded seat terminals.
5. For a direct leaf `check.review` needs only B at every round and `check.repair` is A's step; `check.repair -> merge` is accepted only as an unedited return after a red land exit.
6. The merge queue and land run land it. `commandMerged` records a broadcast only when the repo configures at least one target (every leaf), and for a direct leaf the broadcast prompt goes to the recorded B when B reads idle or done.
7. skills/chart-issues direct-route text describes this flow, and the door's own landing steps and skills/chart-issues/scripts/direct-guards.ts are removed.

Blocked by tool-cgroup-kill (issues/open/seat-shell-kill/tool-cgroup-kill, which replaced seat-turn-idle/shell-sweep on 2026-11-25): this leaf consumes its phase-advance tool-shell kill and keeps it off a direct leaf's seats.

## Why

Tamdoma/akrogon#153. A direct attempt writes no leaf, so `akrogon status` shows only a chart row, its runs record slug `checkout`, and its landing and broadcast are the door's prose steps with no record that they happened. The operator decision (Ivan Juras, 2026-11-23) says direct-route work is tracked, shown and broadcast exactly like a lifecycle leaf, run in the door's current tab by slot A with whichever slot is the reviewer. One real leaf record reaches every existing reader and removes the second landing engine instead of mirroring it.

## Done-criteria

1. Create (decision: tracked like a lifecycle leaf). Inside the existing hc test core in a temp repo, after the door's four files exist under `issues/open/<owner>/<slug>/`, `akrogon direct <slug> --reviewer <address> --source <owner/repo#n>` writes a `state.yaml` that parses under the schema with `direct: true`, `phase: implement`, `created` as a full ISO timestamp, `sources` holding the given identity, `worktree` (an unlocked worktree on branch `<slug>` at the tracking ref, with the leaf env written as for a lifecycle leaf), `frozen: {}`, `frozen_cited: []`, `seat.A` = the calling terminal and `seat.B` = the reviewer, each with the live session that holds the address, and no `tab`. It refuses, writing nothing: outside hc; when no open folder or more than one holds the files without a state; when the owner folder holds another leaf folder; when an owner of that name exists under `issues/closed` or `issues/parked`; when the slug belongs to another open or closed leaf; when the reviewer address is in no live session or in more than one; and when the folder's state has no `direct`. Proof: own test through the real CLI.
2. Rerun. On a direct leaf in `implement` and on one already `merged` whose worktree was removed, a rerun with a new reviewer rewrites only `seat.A` and `seat.B`, clears `delivery_error` for a slot whose address changed, keeps `sources` and `prompted`, and creates no worktree or branch. Proof: own test through the real CLI.
3. Seats untouched (decision: the door's current tab). Driving a direct leaf whose seat refs point at live terminals in the hc test core through `akrogon next` passes in every phase, phase moves into `check.review`, `check.repair`, `check.fix`, `merge`, `merged` and `failed`, `akrogon stop <slug>`, `akrogon stop --all` and a reconcile pass starts, prompts, ends, renames, labels or closes no terminal and ends no process in their scopes, including the phase-advance shell sweep. `seatCommand` refuses a direct leaf. With `max_active: 1` and an active direct leaf, a lifecycle leaf still gets its seat, both with the repo's hc place available and with it refused. `stop --all` stops the direct leaf's live runs. A reconcile pass keeps an unfinished direct leaf's leaf temp folder. Proof: own tests through the real CLI with the hc test core.
4. Status and runs (decision: `akrogon status` shows its phase, seats and runs exactly like a lifecycle leaf). A direct leaf's `akrogon status` row shows its phase and NOTE as a lifecycle leaf's would for the same state, and TIME counted from `created`; `status --json` gives the same start as `first_prompt` and the same `total_ms` in `merges`; `akrogon status <slug>` names the recorded seat terminals in place of the next-start recipe; JSON keeps schema 1. A busy door in `implement` shows `busy A`, also when the seats sit in another hc session than the repo's; after the move to `check.review` the next pass clears `busy A` and records B's busy only. No blocked or stall notice is written for a direct leaf. An `akrogon run-check` and an `akrogon verify` run from the direct leaf's worktree record the leaf's slug, publish their logs under the leaf's `implementation/` folder, and appear on the leaf's status. Proof: own tests through the real CLI.
5. Phases. On a direct leaf, `check.review` completes on B's verdict alone at round 0 and after a fix round; a `fix` verdict moves it to `check.repair`, where `--slot A` moves to `check.fix` and the round count rises by one. `check.repair -> merge` is accepted only after a red land exit with the head equal to the recorded red top, and refused after a `fix` verdict (no land exit), after a gate, conflict or dirty exit, and after an edit. Lifecycle routing is unchanged. `akrogon timing`, for one slug and for a cohort, reports no missing-prompt or `seat_done` gap for a direct leaf's visits. Proof: own tests through the real CLI.
6. Test-change citation. On a direct leaf, a commit adding a test file with no `Test-Change:` trailer refuses the move into `check.review`; the same commit with the trailer passes. Proof: own test through the real CLI.
7. Landing (decision: it broadcasts on landing like a lifecycle leaf). A direct leaf in `merge` with a green receipt lands through the land run in a temp repo with a bare remote: the branch reaches the remote default branch, the leaf moves to `merged`, the owner moves to `issues/closed`, and its `sources` identity is closed with the landed commit, as existing land tests observe through the fake GitHub. Proof: own test through the real CLI.
8. Broadcast. `commandMerged` records `state.broadcast` for a leaf when the repo's broadcast config lists at least one webhook target, and records none when the config is absent or the list is empty. For a direct leaf, a merge pass with B reading idle or done sends the existing broadcast prompt, naming the leaf's sources, to B's recorded address and records `prompted.B`, starting no seat; with B working it sends nothing and status keeps `broadcast pending`; a failed send follows the lifecycle delivery-error settlement. A reconcile pass keeps the merged leaf's cleanup held while B's broadcast turn runs, also when B sits in another hc session than the repo's, and releases it after that turn ends. Proof: own tests through the real CLI with the hc test core.
9. Drop. `akrogon direct-drop <slug> --chart <chart folder>` on a direct leaf in `check.review` with a live run stops that run and its slice, removes the worktree and branch, moves the owner folder with its review, plan, report and run logs to `<chart folder>/direct/<owner>`, and leaves no status row. It refuses a non-direct leaf, a direct leaf in `merge` or `merged`, and an existing `<chart folder>/direct/<owner>`. Proof: own test through the real CLI.
