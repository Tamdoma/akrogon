1. **design.md, launch interface; brief criterion 3:** `seats(global, repo)[slot]` indexes lowercase `{a, b}` with an uppercase lifecycle slot. `Slot` is `'A' | 'B'` (`src/routing.ts:14,20`), and the existing launch explicitly converts it (`src/next.ts:218-219`). The proposed literal interface fails typechecking and would return undefined at runtime. Keep the conversion: `seats(global, repo)[slot === 'A' ? 'a' : 'b']`. The resolver's lowercase keys and the lifecycle's uppercase slots can otherwise stay unchanged.
