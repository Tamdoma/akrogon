# Brief: docs-pictures-concepts

## What

Nine hand-written SVG files in docs/pictures/: factory-overview, two-seats, machine-parts, project-folders, grounding, config-layers, phases, ways-to-run, chart-door, each drawing exactly the nodes and edges its spec lists.

## Why

The rewritten docs show a picture wherever a new concept is introduced (svg-set Q1). These nine explain the system and its parts; later leaves embed them.

## Done-criteria

1. Each of the nine files exists in docs/pictures/ and draws the nodes, edges and roles its spec names, checked by seat B against the spec's named sources (no role, step or combination the source does not support).
2. Every picture listed under What passes the picture check in the design: the reduced-motion screenshots right after load and later both show the complete still picture, moving pictures show motion without the setting, and all labels read at 800px and 360px on light and dark backgrounds; screenshot paths are in the implementation report.
