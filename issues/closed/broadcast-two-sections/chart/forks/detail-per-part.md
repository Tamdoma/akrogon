# How much detail an issue or epic gets

## Question
Q2 · Does the message keep one bullet per shipped part, or get a fixed small size regardless of how much shipped?

### Carries
- Operator: display useful information without overwhelming the user. Readers are beginners across marketing, development, design and account management.
- Current rule in SKILL.md: detail scales with the work, the sender splits long messages at section boundaries, never shorten the truth to fit.

## Findings
- An epic of many leaves produces one bullet per leaf under each section. With two sections that is roughly two thirds of today's length.
- A hard cap forces the writer to merge parts and drop outcomes, which conflicts with "never shorten the truth".

## Taken
Operator 2026-09-14, verbatim: "2b - look at the epic and longer work rules. It should scale with work, but only if needed. That's up to the agent to decide. It's about how easy it is to read for the broad spectrum of people. But yes, for small issues, small bullets".
Effective answer: no fixed size and no mandatory bullet per shipped part. The writer scales the post to what a non-developer needs to understand the outcome, merging parts when that reads better, and keeps small issues to a few short bullets. Foreclosed: the current "one bullet per shipped part" rule and a hard cap.
