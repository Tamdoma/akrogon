## Question
Q1 · When the completed work adds something new rather than fixing something, what does `Before` say?

### Carries
- Operator: titles keep their format and date. Sections become `Before` and `Now`. `Now` states what is better about the system.

## Findings
- The sender requires every section to have at least one bullet. Making `Before` optional means two message shapes and a schema branch.

## Taken
