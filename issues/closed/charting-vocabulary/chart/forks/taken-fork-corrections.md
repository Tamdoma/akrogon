# How is a taken fork corrected before handoff?

## Question
Q5 When you change your mind on a taken fork before handoff, is the original left verbatim and a new fork recorded that names the one it supersedes, with only the effective answer becoming a binding decision?

### Carries
- Intake: a taken fork is never reopened.
- SKILL.md Decide: a late mechanism or contract change gets one B check before A records it. assets/shapes.md: after handoff a contract change is new intake.

## Findings
- (both) The skill has no rule for a pre-handoff correction; the intake's immutability rule needs one or a correction has nowhere to go.
- (B) Keeping every old answer without naming supersession can copy contradictory binding decisions into a self-contained design.

## Taken
Operator, round 1: "5a". Original stays verbatim, a new fork names the one it supersedes, only the effective answer becomes a binding decision. Reason: keeps the record and the "never reopened" rule. Closed: editing a taken fork in place.
