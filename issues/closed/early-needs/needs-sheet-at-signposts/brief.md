# Brief: needs-sheet-at-signposts

## What

The chart-issues skill asks every foreseeable operator-only need and authorization in one needs sheet per door session, completed with the signposts round before the first map, in manual, autopilot and full autopilot alike, its rows answered only by the operator. The rows are recorded as a `## Needs` list, its own section after `## Taken` in the session's `forks/signposts.md` original, one line per row with status (pending, supplied, late), re-copied verbatim to the session's charts. (A,C) Charting proceeds while rows are pending; only the affected proofs and the handoff wait. A need or wider authority found after the signposts round is a late find: the door continues a useful route inside recorded authority, otherwise the chart parks and the door writes one terminal statement and runs one existing `akrogon notice --attention` naming the chart, the blocker and the record path. Every restatement of these rules in skills/chart-issues/SKILL.md (`## Open`, `## Take`, `## Drain`, `## Autopilot`), skills/chart-issues/assets/questions.md, skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md, docs/use/autopilot.md, docs/use/plan-work.md and docs/reference/limits.md says the same. No command or code changes.

## Why

Tamdoma/akrogon#129: in full-autopilot door sessions, A found operator-only needs (a Cloudflare token with account id and domain, two paid-run grant approvals, an operator yes to a close) partway through charting and parked each chart on a `wait:` line seen only in pass-end summaries; the operator did not see them for days. The current skill requires that: `## Take` shows the needs sheet "after the forks settle", grants are recorded during charting, and `## Autopilot`'s first-reply list excludes approvals and lists needs and approvals as chart waits. The operator's direction (2026-11-05): even on full autopilot, A foresees every need the charting will require and asks before charting, at the signposts round at the latest, nothing after.
