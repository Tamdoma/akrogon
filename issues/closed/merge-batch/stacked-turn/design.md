# Design: stacked-turn

## Binding decisions, verbatim
### Merge batching (issues/chart/overnight-recs/CHART.md D5)
Operator 2026-10-15, after the door's overnight report recommended keeping batching parked: "Whay would you keep merging parked?" Earlier the same week: "can we do something about waiting for merges to merge one at a time? Something to parallelize that is simple, elegant, and won't create merge conflicts?" and "No caps at all", "I dont want any slowdowns", "Simplest systemic solution, always work towards simplicity, even removing code if necessary".
Decision: all-or-holder batching (B,E implement; D park unless overridden; consult files issues/chart/overnight-recs/slots/answer-*.md, rebuttal-*.md). Stack the holder and every waiting compatible leaf; green pushes all; red falls back to the holder alone; a head seen in a red stack is not stacked again until it changes but stays eligible as holder (B, rebuttal-B.md).
Foreclosed: split or bisection search, a `--culprit` command, `batch_limit` or any stack-size cap, parallel speculative trains (CPU multiplies), waiting for a stack to fill, and in-place mechanical repair on a red stack of more than the holder (rebuttal-B.md F5: it spends two stacked runs and the repair has no clear leaf owner).
Red-skip key (A, from draft-review-D.md F2): the skip is per (head, base), so innocent members of a red stack re-stack on the next turn after main moves instead of draining one by one. Accepted cost: a red stack costs its innocent members one extra verify wall. Combined verification proves the final tree, not each member prefix (answer-B.md I4 Quality); each leaf's own criteria proof and review are unchanged.
