# Brief: b-repair-phase

## What
Add phase `check.repair`, owned by slot B, between review and merge. When both blind initial verdicts are recorded (or B's re-check verdict) and any is `fix`, the command moves the leaf to `check.repair` instead of `check.fix`, without incrementing `fix_rounds` and without the cap check. In `check.repair` B repairs every Fix from the recorded reviews except plan or design changes, missing planned units, required live runs and operator-only items: each behavior fix is its own commit that starts with a committed failing test reproducing the recorded source; docs and command fixes carry before/after evidence; B runs criterion proof and every `checks` command, then moves to `merge`. Work B may not do, or judges too large, B lists in `review-B.md` under `Handed to A` and moves to `check.fix`; only that move increments `fix_rounds`, and at `fix_rounds >= config.fix_rounds` it goes to `failed` with cause `attempts`, failure phase `check.repair` and reason "fix rounds exhausted". (A,C) Open operator actions follow the `operator-only-items` rule: B repairs the doable Fixes first, then one `failed` stop. A red test or check in `check.repair` with no cause in the leaf's diff takes the same base-run disposition as check.review (`skills/check-issue/SKILL.md:55`): red on base stops with `failed`, green on base is repair work. (B) When A's `check.fix` comes from `check.repair`, A repairs only the `Handed to A` items; a merge-origin repair (`skills/merge-issue/SKILL.md:41`) and a leaf already in `check.fix` before this lands keep today's inputs (B). After a `check.repair` to `check.fix` trip `fix_rounds >= 1`, so the `check.review` re-check is B-only; a merge-origin repair at `fix_rounds` 0 and a recovery into `check.review` at 0 still ask A and B, as today. (A,B,C)

Routing becomes `check.review` next `merge | check.repair | failed`; `check.repair` skill `check-issue`, slots `B`, next `merge | check.fix | failed`; `failed` recovery may target `check.repair`. `merge` to `check.fix` stays as today. Consumes from `operator-only-items`: the `Operator actions` rule in `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` Shared context, referenced from the `check.repair` section. (C)

## Why
Today every Fix, however small, costs a full A turn and a B re-check (`src/routing.ts:31-32`, `src/phase.ts:231-240`). Recent framework history: 35 repair trips since 09-29, 77-94% of them repairable by the reviewer; all 5 recent akrogon trips were one-line local fixes. This removes those trips while A keeps the work only A should do. Consumes from `operator-only-items`: the single `Operator actions` rule in `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` Shared context, which the `check.repair` section references.
