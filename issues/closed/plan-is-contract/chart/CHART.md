# Chart: plan.md is the implementation contract

## Destination
A leaf's `plan.md` is the only whole-leaf execution contract. B implements from it, delegated units get a worker sub-brief from the template, and one completion record carries the evidence. check-issue and merge-issue read the plan and the record. `implementation/brief.md` is written by no skill and named by no guide line. One prose leaf in akrogon over implement-issue, check-issue, brief-template.md and three guide and index lines; old leaves keep their files.
