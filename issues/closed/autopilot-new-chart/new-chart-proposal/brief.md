# Brief: new-chart-proposal

## What

Under autopilot, when a handed chart cannot hand off without a separate destination outside its handed intake, A writes a ready yes/no proposal for that new chart into the waiting chart's CHART.md and parks the chart as an operator approval wait, then moves on within the handed list. The operator's answer in the door pane resumes it: yes adds the proposed chart to the handed list, no brings the found work into the waiting chart. Skill and guide text only: `skills/chart-issues/SKILL.md`, `skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md`, `docs/use/autopilot.md`.

## Why

`skills/chart-issues/SKILL.md` Drain says "Write one chart folder per destination", while `## Autopilot` says the door never extends the handed list and, under autopilot, Drain imports nothing new. When a handed chart turns out to need a separate new chart, nothing says which rule wins, so A guesses and two runs can guess differently. The operator chose to keep control of what autopilot works on (O1) and to receive a ready proposal so the morning decision is one word (chart issues/chart/autopilot-new-chart, forks/hold-proposal.md).

## Done-criteria

1. Trigger and precedence: `SKILL.md` `## Autopilot` states that A parks a handed chart for a new-chart proposal only when the found work is its own destination (its own observable outcome), the handed chart cannot hand off without it, and no existing open leaf or chart already covers it, judged with the research the chart already did; that work the handed chart does not need stays recorded only, a dependency serving the same outcome stays a prerequisite leaf, and splitting expressly handed intake at the map stays A's; that when an existing open chart already covers the found work, A writes no proposal, the waiting chart's `wait:` line names that chart, the chart resumes once that chart's leaves are written with the same ordering as after a yes, and A takes that chart next when it is on the handed list and runnable, otherwise moving to another runnable handed chart; and that under autopilot the handed list wins over writing one chart folder per destination.
2. The wait: `SKILL.md` `## Autopilot` names the new-chart proposal among the things that make a chart wait, as an operator approval that is not a grant; A parks as soon as the proposal can be stated, with findings recorded, and takes no further fork in that chart; nothing is created for the proposed chart (no folder, seed or file) before the operator's yes; the waiting chart's Destination does not change while it waits; consultant agreement never answers the proposal.
3. The answer: `SKILL.md` `## Autopilot` states the proposal's question as "chart <X> on its own (yes) or bring it into <waiting chart> (no)?". A yes is the operator's words adding <X> to the handed list, recorded as a `changed list` mode entry; A charts <X> in the same autopilot run, handover as usual, and the waiting chart resumes after <X>'s leaves are written, handing off with `blocked-by` on them when the dependent leaf shares their destination repo. A no makes A resume the waiting chart with the found work in scope as forks or prerequisite leaves, rewriting the Destination under that answer and recording the operator's no verbatim. Any other operator words stay a valid answer.
4. Cross-repo: `SKILL.md` `## Autopilot` states that when the waiting chart's dependent leaf has a different destination repo than <X>'s leaves, the waiting chart stays unhanded after <X> is handed off, with one `wait:` line naming every needed <repo>/<leaf> of <X> and its landed check (each named leaf folder is under that repo's `issues/closed/`), and that the operator's next message in the same door resumes it; no cross-repo `blocked-by` and no timer.
5. Record: `skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md` `## Autopilot` gains a `- proposal:` line shape holding the proposed destination, its goal, the criterion that cannot pass without it, the waiting chart and the fixed question, one line per proposed destination, written beside that chart's `wait:` line.
6. Guide: `docs/use/autopilot.md` "What still waits for you" lists a needed new chart, explained in plain words with the yes and no effects, and "Read the record" mentions the proposal line.
7. `git --no-pager diff --name-only "$AKROGON_BASE"...HEAD` lists only the three files above.
