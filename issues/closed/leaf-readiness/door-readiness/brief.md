# Brief: door-readiness

## What
The chart-issues door writes a `readiness.yaml` for every leaf it hands off, in the shape `readinessSchema` parses. This leaf consumes readiness-contract's merged schema and `akrogon status` validation, hence blocked-by readiness-contract. `skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md` gains the `readiness.yaml` file with a complete example and the rule that it is written with brief and design before `state.yaml`. `skills/chart-issues/SKILL.md` Take and Handoff replace the prose credential list (SKILL.md:55) with the taken procedure: after forks settle and before proof calls the door shows one key sheet of every need across the proposed leaves, the operator completes it at their pace, and the door checks presence on its draft `readiness.yaml` files with readiness-contract's exported `readReadiness` and `gaps` (`akrogon status` cannot see drafts without `state.yaml`) and runs the proofs. (A,B) A live-change grant is recorded before the first mutating proof and confirmed at handoff review. Proof fixtures get a create/use/delete proof with the declared identities and an authenticated absence read-back, and are retained only by prior agreement. A producer's save operation is proven per save-route. Handoff stores steps, grants, fixtures, retained resources, produced values and proofs in the leaf's `readiness.yaml`.

## Why
The brief template has no credentials section, and one brief called a token "Already set" with no scope list, then failed on a missing DNS scope (Tamdoma/akrogon#52, I2, P5). The operator chose a door-written contract, one key sheet completed before proofs, grants recorded at charting, disposable fixtures with proven cleanup, and proven producer saves.
