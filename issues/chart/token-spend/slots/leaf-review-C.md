# Leaf review C: lean-exchange

Disagreements only. Brief and design below mean slots/leaf-draft/lean-exchange/brief.md and design.md.

D1. Criterion 1 is confounded by criteria 5 and 6. Criteria 2 and 3 are scoped to "that branch run" (brief.md:14-15), criterion 6 forces "a forced compaction of branch A mid-chart" (brief.md:18), and nothing places criterion 5's timed-out wait and rerun outside the measured pair (brief.md:17). Compaction alone drops A to its restore floor, and forcing a peer busy to time out its wait puts an extra turn inside the measured span. Either event can move the branch total at brief.md:13 for reasons unrelated to the three changes, and the base run gets neither event. As implementer I cannot attribute the delta. Criteria 5 and 6 need their own exchanges outside both measured runs.

D2. Criterion 2 fails on a legitimate budget rerun. It allows one A tool call per exchange step (brief.md:14), but a `budget` outcome obliges A to rerun peer-wait as a second call (skills/chart-issues/assets/questions.md:46: every other `budget` "is rerun at once"; unproven idle or done is reported first). A slow consultant in the live run therefore breaks criterion 2 with the leaf working as designed. Scope it to exchanges collected within the first peer-ask budget, or count budget reruns separately.

D3. Criterion 7 names the wrong printer and leaves a gap against criterion 2. peer-ask.ts prints no outcome line: it spawns peer-wait with inherited stdout (skills/chart-issues/scripts/peer-ask.ts:78-81), and the line is printed at skills/chart-issues/scripts/peer-wait.ts:239. Criterion 7 as written (brief.md:19) covers only peer-ask's lines, while a direct peer-wait rerun also ends an exchange. If rerun lines carry no session, A needs the separate `herdr agent list` call criterion 2 forbids after an exchange. Design flags this as open (design.md:17), but the criterion must require the session on every outcome line however produced, and design.md:13 should name tests/peer-wait.test.ts as a proof home.

D4. peer-restart can print a stale codex session as the new one. Criterion 7 asks for "the new session... when herdr shows one" (brief.md:19) and design.md:18 calls it "the one herdr shows after start". After a codex restart herdr keeps the old session id until an exchange (issues/open/door-handover/door-reset/readiness.yaml:22). A post-start list read therefore returns a non-null value that is the previous session, and the door would append it under the same seat letter, where SKILL.md:29 exists precisely to forbid wrong-session entries (null is only the named case). Neither file states the guard the implementer needs: print none when the post-start session equals the pre-restart session.

D5. Criterion 6's content comparison has no executable mechanism. The restored copy is truncated text inside A's context, not a file, and no owned surface records its hash or size. To "compare content" (design.md:11) A must read the current file, which is the spend criterion 6 (brief.md:18) counts against it, or compare by a proxy the leaf never names (mtime, byte size, spot-check). The timing is also unspecified: "changed after the restore" needs change measured against the restored snapshot at A's first post-compaction read, not detection at an arbitrary later moment. Name the comparison the skill text will instruct, or record it as Open for the plan in design.md:16-18.
