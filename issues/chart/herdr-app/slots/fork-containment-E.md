## Q2. How: wrap argv in `systemd-run --user --scope` at start, or move `hc`'s `pid` after start (`adoptShell`)?

1. **Pick:** **Wrap the leaf seat's harness argv in `systemd-run --user --scope` at start:**
   `env DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS=<bus> systemd-run --user --scope --quiet --expand-environment=no --unit=<scope> --slice=<leafSlice> -p TimeoutStopSec=5 -p CollectMode=inactive-or-failed -p SendSIGHUP=yes -- env <seat env...> <harness argv...>`
   - **Reason:**
     1. **Eliminates the child-escape race:** In old herdr, `adoptShell` moved an idle `bash` shell *before* `herdr agent start` ran. Under `hc`, `hc run --bg` starts the agent and its first task in one step (`delivery` `Taken` Q1 1a). As seen in `hc-hold-105b0ede.service`, `codex` forks `codex-code-mode-host` and `cua-repl` immediately at launch; Linux cgroup v2 `StartTransientUnit` (`PIDs=[pid]`) moves only `pid` itself, leaving already-forked children stranded in `hc-hold-*.service`. `systemd-run --user --scope` enters `<leafSlice>/<scope>.scope` *before* `execvp`ing the harness, containing 100% of children from birth.
     2. **Keeps `terminal.pid === harness` and removes polling:** Both `env` and `systemd-run --scope` `execvp` in place, so `hc`'s `pid` and `foreground.pid` stay the harness process (`session-id` `Taken` Q2 works unchanged), while deleting `adoptShell`, `paneShell`, the 50ms `while` polling loop in [`src/next.ts:626-631`](file:///home/ivan/Work/infra/akrogon/src/next.ts#L626-L631) (`forks/signposts.md` "no polling or waiting"), and [`tests/fake-busctl.ts`](file:///home/ivan/Work/infra/akrogon/tests/fake-busctl.ts).
   - **Cost:** Simplify `inOwnScope` ([`src/scope.ts:191-208`](file:///home/ivan/Work/infra/akrogon/src/scope.ts#L191-L208)) to check `cgroup` against `/${slice}/<scope>.scope` using `pid` from `hc get`, and use `tests/fake-systemd-run.sh` instead of `tests/fake-busctl.ts`.
2. **Rejected options:**
   - **Post-start `adoptShell` (`busctl StartTransientUnit` on `pid` from `hc get`):** Rejected. Races with early child forks during `hc run --bg` (stranding startup children in `hc-hold-*.service`) and keeps the 50ms `Bun.sleep(50)` polling loop in `src/next.ts:626-631` plus `tests/fake-busctl.ts`.
