## 2. Rejected options

- **O1 Needs me as the section name, or all idle/no-busy seats as attention.** The envelope does not encode a general approval/question or live idle state. Between-seat transitions would become false requests for operator action.
- **O2 Existing leaf.red as the entire final definition.** It is a good shared base, but presently misses broken dependency references. Keep the authoritative calculation in the producer and add those specific facts there, rather than inventing a second policy in the mod.
- **O3 Promote every dependent of a failed or parked leaf.** One owner problem would fill the attention section with downstream waiting rows. Name that owner on each dependent, and show its own failure once where it belongs. Missing/unreadable owners differ because their own task row may not exist.
- **O4 A separate Between seats/Ready/Queued section.** A dispatch gap does not need another heading. “Queued” also overclaims a recorded queue except for actual merge-queue entries. Waiting plus the reason is sufficient.
- **O5 Red after N minutes without another fact.** Duration can prompt inspection but proves neither a stall nor human need. This would add a scheduling policy and require a separate threshold decision. The watch rule judges concrete looping/error/hung-tool evidence, not age alone.
- **O6 Stopped always in attention, or holds always overriding errors.** The first treats an explicit operator stop as damage. The second hides an existing failure. Attention first, with the hold preserved as a fact, handles both.
- **O7 Recently done, or merged tasks in Waiting because their epic remains open.** The former conflicts with the removal lock. The latter calls completed work unfinished. Explicit pending follow-up is the narrow exception, not a history section.
- **O8 One all-problems header number.** It mixes task rows, unreadable files and deployment records with different counting units. The task-only label is easier to interpret. Do not force the dot off when the task count is zero.
- **O9 Multiple copies of a task under different sections.** It inflates counts and separates two views of the same row. Use one section with secondary facts and persistent expansion/pinning.

## 4. Pitfalls and what removes them

- **R1 Stale busy records override explicit holds.** Classify the hold before normal activity and retain the hold reason. Never infer a live process merely from a timestamp surviving stop.
- **R2 Completed dependencies remain blockers.** Exclude label merged from unresolved dependencies. Verify a leaf whose entire blocked_by list is merged reaches normal Working/Waiting assignment.
- **R3 Attention differs by surface.** Compute its task facts once in the producer and consume leaf.red. Keep the broader envelope.red meaning explicit. Independent fetches can still show different snapshots briefly; do not add a second classifier to chase that timing difference.
- **R4 One problem becomes many alleged human requests.** Keep readable failed/parked dependency owners separate from their Waiting dependents, and keep “Needs attention” distinct from “Needs me”. Count a task once even with several reasons.
- **R5 Category movement loses the inspected task.** Stable repo/slug keys own expansion and pins, as required by list-shape Taken. No section-name component in task identity.
- **R6 Merged filtering hides explicit unfinished follow-up.** Evaluate concrete attention and pending-broadcast facts before hiding clean merged rows. Do not invent full broadcast-completion tracking from the current flag.
- **R7 Missing repositories disappear with their absent task rows.** Keep read/deploy/scan problems outside task classification. Their visibility must not depend on a task existing or a section being nonempty.
- **R8 Required-seat guesses call a completed seat missing.** Use routing/requiredSlots and done if naming the pending seat. A generic “Next step pending” needs no duplicate routing table in the renderer. No general live-idle claim is supported by the current envelope.
- **R9 Parked entries are not full live-leaf records.** `jsonRepo` supplies parked folder names only. Show their repo/slug and parked fact without inventing seat assignment, plan/worktree paths or usable Files/Changes targets. Rich parked-row inspection would require more producer data.

Implementation proof should cover held+busy, attention+busy, all dependencies merged, missing/unreadable dependency, initial review versus review after a fix, between-seat gap, clean merged child, pending-broadcast child, and deploy-only error with zero attention tasks. These are classification checks, not exact-word/pixel tests. No implementation or tests were changed for these notes.

## 5. Additional question

**Q1 Should Tasks track post-merge broadcast work through completion, or only its pending dispatch?** The current flag stops being true when B is prompted, so it cannot support a promise that the row remains until the broadcast finishes. My default is pending-dispatch visibility only, with clean merged work omitted. Full follow-up tracking needs a producer-contract decision, not a Recently done section.
