1. **F1 — No ledger is workable only with an explicit unknown-history rule.** Item 6's existing records do not support its repeat-detection promise. `busy_notified` records the core elapsed-busy notification, not a lead notice or its evidence (`src/next.ts:192-208`). Recovery deletes the failure and resets attempts/fix rounds (`src/phase.ts:98-111`); the move log does not store failure.reason or lead notices (`src/log.ts:18-30`). After compaction, “same failure with no new evidence” cannot safely mean “I do not remember seeing it.” Keep the no-ledger choice, but require the compaction carry to retain outstanding anomaly evidence and recovery outcomes. When that history cannot be established from the carry or existing artifacts, suspend automatic recovery and do not claim an anomaly is new. Unlimited judged recovery then remains evidence-based instead of repeatedly resetting the command's exhaustion limits.

2. **F2 — Recovery lost the all-seats-quiescent prerequisite.** Item 4(b) permits recovery based on the failed reason and one seat pane, without checking whether a peer still works. A failed move preserves panes/worktree, and recovery resets done/verdict/attempts for both seats (`src/phase.ts:95-111`). A working peer could therefore finish against a restarted phase. Restore the requirement to inspect all required peers and confirm quiescence plus fresh phase state before recovery. Unlimited recovery does not require abandoning that concurrency boundary.

3. **F3 — “All open leaves are merged” is not proof completion bookkeeping succeeded.** A transition saves merged before completeOwner runs (`src/phase.ts:113-125`). completeOwner can fail on an existing destination or source closing before moving the issue into closed (`:146-171`). Item 5 would then delete the watch with merged leaves still stranded in open. `next` handles a merged leaf through completeOwner (`src/next.ts:459-461`); cleanup skips leaves still in open and operates on closed worktrees (`:487-493,557-565`). Before stopping, require a successful inventory and confirmed completion bookkeeping, or explicitly report the outstanding completion/cleanup error rather than saying everything is done. Treat malformed/unreadable inventory as an error, never an empty tree. If cleanup is part of the promised completion, perform/check its existing command path before stopping; a moved closed inventory alone does not prove cleanup ran.

4. **F4 — “Already announced” conflates an attempted notification with delivery.** Item 6 suppresses notices for human-blocked failures on the assumption they were announced, and item 5 can stop on that basis. `failure.delivery` can be `error`, and records Herdr's returned reason even when not shown (`src/phase.ts:48-71`; `issues/closed/noninteractive-leaf-execution/failure-attention/brief.md:4`). Specify the distinction between attempted, shown and undelivered. A human-only failure is not a recovery decision, so the rule “notify only when it took a recovery decision” also excludes the main case needing notification. Report an undelivered required-action notice and its outcome without manufacturing a recovery or claiming the operator was notified.

5. **F5 — A bare `/watch-issues` cron prompt lacks stable repo/job identity.** Item 3 matches any job with that text and item 6 relies on session context. That cannot distinguish watches for different registered repos in one lead conversation, and a changed cwd can redirect the action: `next --all` selects by current cwd, falling back to every registered repo outside one (`src/next.ts:610-617`). Put the authoritative repo root and necessary target identity in the scheduled prompt, and match/delete only that watch's job. This uses the existing cron record and needs no ledger. Distinguish direct activation from a scheduled tick so a tick never recreates its expired/deleted job. Do not create a new job after the initial check has already satisfied the stop condition.
