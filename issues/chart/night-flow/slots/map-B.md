## 2. Stocks, flows and feedback

**S1. Unmerged work.** Admission and repair add work to the ready queue. Only a successful checked push drains it. Framework's hold made that drain zero while three further merge arrivals accumulated. Faster implementation alone would enlarge the stock. **S2. Failed work.** Counter exhaustion and contract blockers add to it. Ordinary dispatch supplies no outflow: `A:src/next.ts:704–706` skips failed leaves, and `src/phase.ts:241–244` rejects seat recovery. The operator is the remaining actuator. **S3. Verification debt.** Missing, disposable or self-authored proof adds unresolved outcomes, repeated runs and review work. Formspark's missing artifacts and harness-written offers.yaml demonstrate this stock (`review-B.md:380–400`). **S4. Deployment lag.** Landed program changes accumulate behind a mutable installed checkout that cannot fast-forward (`A:src/self-update.ts:41–58`).

## 8. Lifetime pitfalls and removal

- **R1. Guards strand old live state.** Remove the rollout mismatch by owning migration/config changes with enforcement, proving both existing and future worktrees. Do not disturb live panes, uncommitted work or an applied merge stack. Existing reconciler criteria own those protections.
- **R2. “No failures” becomes weaker proof.** Preserve criteria and complete per-unit coverage. Formspark's runner-generated production output and env's passing invalid-refusal tests show why green totals alone are insufficient. Root-state sentinel failure and incomplete core runs still need honest disposition.
- **R3. Automatic recovery becomes infinite repeated work.** Keep the failure evidence and existing review of actual progress. Forbid exhausted live mutations, unchanged retries and cross-scope repair. Do not reset lifetime accounting when a session is replaced.
- **R4. Deployment publishes or destroys operator work.** Separate runtime from records, or prove issue-only committed history and lock order before automatic sync. Keep a clear deployed revision and preserve unknown local edits. A blanket auto-rebase/push is not justified by #80.
- **R5. Proof vanishes during cleanup.** Put durable, revision-bound evidence in the leaf holder before disposing consumers. Formspark's absent reports demonstrate the failure. Existing recording-consumers/check-scratch own lifecycle cleanup, so reuse their contracts rather than create another evidence daemon.
- **R6. A watchdog is added without settling its tradeoff.** Current events already know about these terminal failures. Fix those transitions first. Pure-silence detection remains the explicitly accepted limit unless Q3 changes it. More alerts cannot perform a withheld recovery or supply a grant.

Highest-value order: **unblock the migration rollout, repair the env serialization outcome, finish differential gating, and remove counter-created dead ends.** Faster leaf production is secondary until the stopped outflows work.
