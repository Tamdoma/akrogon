**Q1 — May a page have no client referral?** Options: **A, recommend:** zero-or-one contextual referral when useful, citations retain their own provenance and explicit budget treatment. **B:** retain one referral everywhere and only change presentation. A removes the mandatory-footer problem across #72/#77/#80/#81. Research: allocator/render code above and Google S2. This changes the merged plan contract, so record an explicit new decision.

**Q2 — Should research freeze coherent page records or independent slug pools?** **A, recommend:** freeze subject/title/slug/parent-group records and let scripts deterministically select a coherent subset. **B:** keep independent pools and have an agent repair IA after selection. A removes mismatch at its source and extends the existing QuestionRecord mechanism (#68/#75). Research: `generate-sitemap.ts:283,291,443`. Pitfall: select parents that cover chosen leaves, and preserve published routes during updates instead of re-slugging them.

**Q3 — What backs an About page?** **A, recommend:** real operator-supplied publisher facts, with no personal origin story when none exists. **B:** require sourced author/origin input before every network can proceed. Both avoid invention, A permits useful impersonal editorial pages. Research: persona/about mismatch above, operator identity lock. Pitfall: solo/pair/team variety is not evidence that those people exist. Do not turn duplicate-story removal into synthetic biographies.

**Q4 — How can readers contact every publisher?** **A, recommend:** renderer-owned static Formspark POST form with provisioned per-site IDs. **B:** a real operator-supplied email contact page. **Q5 — How much header behavior is needed?** **A, recommend:** explicit static or small pinned-nav variants, accessible mobile navigation and short labels, tested with long titles, open menus and scrolling. **B:** add compact-on-scroll animation and more interaction variants. A meets the need with fewer states. Research: renderer above and Page Laubheimer S3. Pitfall: “most headers must stick” is not a universal usability rule. Test anchor/focus obstruction, and do not add a second review system or a blocking uncalibrated similarity score.

**Q6 — Where do unreviewed ranked providers link?** **A, recommend:** internal review when available, otherwise the provider’s sourced public destination. **B:** limit the ranked list to reviewed providers.
