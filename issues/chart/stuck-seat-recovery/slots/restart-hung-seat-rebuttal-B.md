# Disagreements

- **F1 · E2/E3 do not establish a hung seat.** An open outside-root confirmation is an intentional permission wait, not restart authority. `~/.pi/agent/extensions/tamdoma-subagents/tools.ts:98` explicitly awaits approval before spawning at line 119. A pending spawn with no worker process also matches normal operation: children are in-process SDK sessions (`~/.pi/agent/extensions/tamdoma-subagents/child-session.ts:692`), not necessarily OS worker processes. Gate clock-free recovery on a demonstrated lost completion path or an owner-reported irrecoverable fault, not E2 or “a pending spawn.” Otherwise the proposed C gate can restart legitimate approval waits or healthy startup. B did not endorse that gate.

- **F2 · “The reliable stop is SIGTERM to the pi pid … then SIGKILL” is not B's conclusion, and “the missing piece is only the stop” drops required work.** The PID returned by process-info lacks birth identity (`/home/ivan/Work/infra/herdrdev/herdr/src/api/schema/panes.rs:441`). Detached worker groups can survive parent termination, including SIGTERM when cleanup does not run. Their command executes before the parent-pipe read (`~/.pi/agent/extensions/tamdoma-subagents/worker-shell.ts:10`), and normal cleanup explicitly kills and confirms the group (`:142`). B requires stable identity, established ownership/containment, confirmed cessation of all writers, and refusal to relaunch when that cannot be established. The recovery operation must also exclude concurrent event-driven dispatch and re-read phase/done after stopping (`/home/ivan/Work/infra/akrogon/src/next.ts:410`, `:682`). A restart counter alone does not supply these guarantees.

- **F3 · Fresh sessions are a proposed choice, not an existing relaunch guarantee.** `/home/ivan/Work/infra/akrogon/src/next.ts:220` passes configured harness arguments through, so launch flags determine whether a session is fresh or resumed. B's round offered exact-session resume as an alternative and recommended fresh reentry only with the old transcript reference, recovery context and reconciliation of partial effects. Restore that material choice and contract. “Worktree and state.yaml survive” alone does not prevent repeating a completed external action.

- **F4 · Remove B's attribution to “every practitioner mechanism … uses elapsed time” and “all detect hangs with elapsed time.”** B's practitioner sources were Young on durable agent context and Featonby on safe retries, neither a hang detector. Erlang supervision reacts to termination events without a silence detector. Its shutdown timeout bounds an already-requested stop, while restart intensity limits recurrence. See [OTP Supervisor Behaviour](https://www.erlang.org/doc/system/sup_princ.html), read 2026-09-28. Conflating these functions obscures the actual decision: permitting bounded stop waits is distinct from permitting silence-triggered detection.
