# Brief: run-count-rule

## What

Change the lifecycle skills' written grant rule so a seat keeps a correct count of covered launches against each repeat bound, warns before a launch past it, and review audits the count. Text only, in `skills/plan-issue/SKILL.md`, `skills/implement-issue/SKILL.md`, `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` and `skills/merge-issue/SKILL.md` ("Shared context" grant sentence; implement-issue "implement" worker rule; check-issue review audit), `skills/chart-issues/SKILL.md` ("Take", the grant paragraph beginning "Each leaf's `grants`"), `skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md` ("readiness.yaml" `grants` placeholders) and `skills/chart-issues/assets/standing-design.md` (the recorded-output line's covering-grant clause).

## Why

Tamdoma/akrogon#126: on framework leaf `fixture-weave-recorder` (2026-11-03) a grant allowed "at most 1 known-good recorder run and 1 rerun"; seat A made at least three paid-reaching recorder runs under the renewed two-run grant (about six against four across its grants, inferred from run durations in framework `issues/timings.jsonl`) and reported "1 run + 1 rerun" (`implementation/report.md` "Initial paid run"). Seat B, under a grant that named its unit ("every invocation that reaches a paid session whatever its outcome"), counted right and stopped. Recurrence of #107, whose fix (e522c186) added the compare-before-launch sentence without a count. The operator chose written rules only and "allow it with a warning" for runs past a bound (session signposts 1c, 2b).

## Done-criteria

1. A reader of the "Shared context" grant rule in each of plan-issue, implement-issue, check-issue and merge-issue learns the same rule: every covered launch runs through `akrogon run-check` and that reaches its paid or live stage counts against its bound's unit, whatever its outcome and whatever the bound's wording ("known-good" never narrows it); one count line per launch goes in the seat's pass artifact before the launch (grant entry, bound, count so far, unit) and is completed after (outcome, IDs, sessions, cost when known); earlier artifacts' lines are read before the first launch; prior use that cannot be established is written as unknown, never as a number.
2. The same rule says that before a launch past a repeat-count bound of a paid operation the seat writes a warning in its pass artifact and its visible reply and continues, while live-change repeat bounds, spend caps, operation, target, identity, effects, expiry and `stop_line` clauses keep their operator-blocker stop; the stop wins when both apply; unknown prior use warns for a repeat count and stops when it prevents establishing compliance with a stop-type bound.
3. A reader of check-issue learns that check.review launches no covered operation (live proofs run in implement and check.repair as today, and a needed live proof missing from the plan is a Fix for check.repair), and that review treats a warned overrun as information with the proof standing, while an unwarned overrun, a count that does not match `check-<name>-*.log` and `issues/timings.jsonl`, or unexplained paid activity is a Fix, and an Operator action is recorded when a stop-type bound or `stop_line` blocks the next launch.
4. A reader of implement-issue "implement" learns that a worker command that can reach a paid session is counted by A, who writes its count line.
5. A reader of chart-issues "Take" grant paragraph learns that each repeat bound names its unit and what consumes one in plain words, nested units are their own bound, `stop_line` never restates a repeat count, a renewal entry states its new total or "N more after <date or commit>", the door's charting probe counts against the leaf grant it runs under, and the door itself never warns and runs: every paid proof call still waits for operator approval. `assets/shapes.md` `bounds` and `stop_line` placeholders show the same.
6. A reader of `assets/standing-design.md` recorded-output line learns that a fresh call past a repeat bound under signpost 2b's warning counts as covered, and a call outside the grant still stops.
7. `git --no-pager diff --name-only "$AKROGON_BASE"...HEAD` lists only the files named in What; `src/`, `tests/` and `issues/` are unchanged.
