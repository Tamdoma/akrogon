# Signposts angles, slot C

Correction to draft 4: "no new enforcement" is already settled by the intake, so it is not a signpost question. Replace it with Q1 below.

## Q1. Who must prove a call is mechanical before a script may decide it?

- O1: The script proposer must name the literal contract or other one-right-answer source. If none is named, it is a model judgment.
- O2: A script is allowed until a reviewer proves the call is a judgment.
- O3: No default. Seats agree case by case.
- Recommended: O1. Most borderline counts have no such source.
- Steers: handoff refusals, use of the keep list, Fix without a failed run, and every fork that proposes a count or text match.

## Q2. May the review seat re-make a recorded judgment, or only test its record?

- O1: Check only that the choice, reason, and evidence are present and support the choice. Do not substitute a different quality call.
- O2: Re-make the quality call and block when the reviewer would choose differently.
- O3: O1 for reversible, low-cost calls. O2 only for high-cost or hard-to-reverse calls.
- Recommended: O3. Full re-judging everywhere creates a second, unwritten threshold.
- Steers: draft 2 proof form, reviewer authority, record detail, and whether known-good and known-bad pairs are needed.

## Q3. Which decisions stay with the operator because they are the operator's, not because a model cannot judge them?

- O1: None beyond spend, destructive effects, and security. The model also decides taste, brand, and public claims and records why.
- O2: The operator keeps taste, brand, public claims, and any new spend or destructive effect. The model decides the rest.
- O3: The operator keeps any call a seat marks sensitive in the leaf, with no fixed list.
- Recommended: O2. It separates ownership from capability and keeps stops rare and named in advance.
- Steers: when an operator step is a valid pitfall remover, autopilot limits, and which per-item stops must be refused.

## Q4. Does the new lens reach work already charted or in flight?

- O1: New charts only. Existing briefs keep their named criteria.
- O2: It applies when an open leaf, check, or lesson next touches the criterion. A named proxy criterion can then be sent back as wrong, not enforced.
- O3: Active sweep of existing checks and open briefs in this session.
- Recommended: O2. O1 leaves the criterion shield in place. O3 turns this chart into the drain work draft 1 rejects.
- Steers: wrong-criterion routing in review, lesson seeding as removal, scope, and whether paid reruns keep being forced by old gates.

## Q5. How much cost may proof of one judgment impose?

- O1: Fixed form for all judgments: recorded real run, per-item reason and evidence.
- O2: Proof and record scale with cost and reversibility. Small reversible calls need a short batch record. Paid or hard-to-reverse calls need a fuller record and run.
- O3: No run required. The written reason alone is the proof.
- Recommended: O2. A fixed heavy form moves the suffocation from scripts into prose and paid reruns.
- Steers: draft 2, plan entries for judgment criteria, recording and replay cost, and how much review reading a leaf must buy.
