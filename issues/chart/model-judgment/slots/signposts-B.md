## S1. Who has the last word when the review seat disagrees with a recorded judgment? (new)

The draft makes the review seat the judge and says nothing about a disagreement. If every disagreement blocks, the pressure moves from scripts to review rounds. The intake's pilot shows this already happened once (11 form-only rounds against 6).

- 1a. The writer's recorded call stands. The reviewer blocks only when it names a concrete harm on the recorded evidence, such as a missed fact or a broken function. A difference in taste is a note.
- 1b. The reviewer's reading always wins and is a Fix.
- 1c. A disagreement goes to the operator.

Recommended: 1a, with at most one repair round on a judgment and no operator stop after it.
Steers: the X1 proof sentence, the X5 verdict wording, repair-round bounds, any convergence fork (copy-judgment-convergence took "bound 2 plus an operator stop").

## S2. Who carries the burden at the boundary, and who may grant an exception? (new)

X1 has two exits: "a value inside a granted bound" and "an operator-named literal contract". Any count can be relabelled as one of them. Under autopilot the door answers forks itself, so the door could name the exception.

- 2a. The check carries the burden. A script decides only when the design names the single right answer. Unclear cases go to the model. A number on model output exists only from the operator's own words, never from a door pick under autopilot.
- 2b. Same burden, and the door may also name a bound under autopilot with a recorded reason.
- 2c. Unclear cases default to a script check.

Recommended: 2a.
Steers: the X1 exception clause, the X2 audit refusal, what `autopilot.md` lets the door pick, Fix against Nit in X5, every later fork where a seat proposes "just a small limit".

## S3. Correction to draft 2: what does the proof prove, and who pays?

"One recorded real run" per judgment criterion adds paid calls, and recordings make gates expensive to remove (loop L4). A new required "reason record" also invites scripts that check its length, format or keywords, which is the same pattern again.

- 3a. The script checks only that the record exists and is not blank. The reviewer reads quality on a recording the leaf already has. A judgment criterion never triggers a paid call by itself.
- 3b. One dedicated recorded run per judgment criterion.
- 3c. A known-good and known-bad pair.

Recommended: 3a.
Steers: the X1 proof sentence, X4 plan entries, the fresh-call conditions in the standing-design recorded-output line, spend grants on the needs sheet.

## S4. Correction to draft 1: who removes an existing proxy check when it blocks a run?

"Do not drain" is right for a sweep. It leaves loop B3 unchanged for checks already in the repos: the operator notices days later. The intake also excludes implement-issue from changes, which fixes the answer before the question is asked.

- 4a. The seat that hits it removes it in the same branch with the reason recorded, and the review seat judges the removal. Keep-list checks are never removable this way.
- 4b. The seat files a seed and stops.
- 4c. Operator only, as today.

Recommended: 4a. The standing-design deletion line ("delete a false or outdated expectation with its reason") already allows it for tests. Known cost: seats may delete to get green (framework#115), so the keep list must be a hard limit.
Steers: whether implement-issue stays excluded, X5, X6 removal seeds, how tight the wording of draft 5 must be.
