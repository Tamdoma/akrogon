## 3. Pitfalls over the work's lifetime
- P1. The standing line becomes a slogan read once and ignored at forks. Remover: option part F1 O1, operative clauses at the fork round and the audit. info-framework.md `## 5` shows proxies were born at forks after a signpost already said "the model decides", so the fork point is the one that must carry text.
- P2. A proxy is relabelled a "granted bound" or a safety check. Remover: leaf done-criterion in the audit wording: the design cites the operator words or standard that supply the value; an uncited bound is refused.
- P3. Reasons degrade to boilerplate ("looks fine"). Remover: option part F3 plus Taken 3a; the reviewer reads the evidence named in the record, and an unsupported reason is a concrete harm.
- P4. Seats delete real checks to get green under Taken 5a. Remover: option part F4 and F6: keep list is never removable this way, removals are judged as a batch, and the removal cites the valid work refused.
- P5. Removing a gate strands recordings that exercised it, forcing paid re-records. Remover: leaf done-criterion under Taken 2a: state the recording unproven or let the next natural run replace it; no paid call for the removal alone.
- P6. Review disagreement rounds multiply in place of script rounds. Remover: option part F4 O1, taste stays a Nit and opens no repair.
- P7. A future chart re-adds the known-good/known-bad pair as "just examples". Remover: door probe: the audit refusal covers any expected value held for a choice, examples included; record-part cases stay allowed.
