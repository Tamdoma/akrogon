# Slot B disagreements

R1. The operator-message windows proposed at `slots/proof-of-saving-merged.md:20` do not establish fork or handoff boundaries. A restatement, correction or direct peer request is also an operator message. Splitting there can divide one fork across rows, and including seat output merely by timestamp does not identify which task produced it. Evidence: `forks/proof-of-saving.md:12,19` distinguishes restatement requests from rounds and says usefulness requires judgment. My Pick requires boundaries linked to exchange prompts/messages or artifacts, not inferred from every operator message. Automated windows are useful raw data, but A must associate them with forks and identify the map/handoff spans. Otherwise label them operator-message intervals, not time or usage per fork.

R2. Tokens and minutes are insufficient to establish “cheaper” when models change (`slots/proof-of-saving-merged.md:22`). Usage classes are comparable accounting quantities, but their dollar weights differ. Evidence: the model-specific baselines and planned operator model practice at `forks/proof-of-saving.md:10-11,18`. I did not propose a hardcoded model price table. My notes permit an explicitly sourced, dated rate estimate or unmeasured dollars. A caller-supplied accounting rate does not select or fix the peer's model. Tokens-only reporting is an operator choice, but its conclusion must remain “token usage changed,” not “dollar cost fell.” Retain the dollars question and that limitation.

R3. The merged quality/comparison proposal at `slots/proof-of-saving-merged.md:23` removes two parts of my pick: showing substantive defects alongside usage, and presenting the initial three-chart comparison without waiting for another request. A restatement count cannot reveal a lost dependency or incomplete contract. Evidence: `slots/proof-of-saving-B.md`, Pick and Pitfalls, requires A's substantive review, visible defects and a three-chart comparison, while explicitly rejecting automatic quality grades and causal claims. Preserve that difference. I oppose claiming success from the figures alone, even with model/effort differences named.

R4. Reproducing today's hand figures “within a stated tolerance” (`slots/proof-of-saving-merged.md:27`) is insufficient accounting validation. Both my notes and D5 (`:24`) identify duplicate Claude blocks and cumulative Codex counters as concrete hazards. A total near a manual total can conceal compensating errors. Use small independently totaled fixtures for those cases, missing boundaries and counter resets, then reconcile the historical examples after their boundaries and deduplication are checked. Integer token arithmetic should match exactly for identical records and boundaries. Any tolerance belongs to a named estimate or attribution uncertainty, not unspecified parser accuracy.
