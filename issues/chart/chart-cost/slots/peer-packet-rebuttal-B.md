# Slot B disagreements

R1. O5's “a peer needs nothing from earlier turns” (`slots/peer-packet-merged.md:20`) is not my position. The brief supplies the current task, but the peer's independent position remains relevant during rebuttal and recovery. My pick retains sessions and access to the peer's own notes, rather than requiring every brief to reproduce that history. Evidence: `slots/peer-packet-B.md`, Q2 Pick, Rejected and Pitfalls. The merged file itself recognizes that resetting before rebuttal removes needed context (`:23`). Keep the common pick, but remove the claim that prior context is unnecessary.

R2. “Any reset costs about what it saves” and “net saving at most a few tenths of a dollar per chart” (`slots/peer-packet-merged.md:21,24`) are unsupported counterfactuals. The measured $1.60 covers all later cache reads; a reset need not remove all those reads, and resetting can change cache writes, retrieval, output and compaction costs. Fresh startup at $0.90 and one observed compaction at $0.81 do not establish the net result of either alternative. Evidence: `forks/peer-packet.md:16-18` and my Q2 Evidence explicitly mark reset savings unmeasured. I reject a mandatory reset because no saving is demonstrated, not because its maximum saving has been measured. Likewise, reopening when cache-read share exceeds one reset's cost (`slots/peer-packet-merged.md:39`) needs a full-fork counterfactual comparison, not that single-cost comparison.

R3. Compaction during a peer turn is not “removed by peer-wait's failure outcome” (`slots/peer-packet-merged.md:37`). Evidence: `skills/chart-issues/assets/questions.md:44` defines failure as an idle or finished peer with a missing or empty return file. It does not detect compaction or lost reasoning while a peer continues and returns a non-empty file. Durable peer notes support recovery, but also do not guarantee that dissent survives (`slots/peer-packet-merged.md:36`). Describe those measures as recovery support, without claiming they remove compaction risk.

R4. The brief is not “the only evidence the blind lock was kept” (`slots/peer-packet-merged.md:7`). It records what A supplied, not everything the peer inspected. Evidence: the blind rule in `skills/chart-issues/assets/questions.md:48` excludes other peer work, while the replay separately audited tool calls for contamination (`slots/map-size-test.md:25`). A retained brief is useful dispatch evidence. It cannot alone establish blind execution, and transcripts can supply additional evidence.
