- **1a (recommended)** (B,C) I write a short summary file instead: each option, who picked it, its reason and cost, the evidence, each risk with the step that removes it, any open question, and where we differ. The helpers check that. Then I write the full round once, in chat, for you. It wins because there is one copy instead of two, and every choice, number and risk is still in front of the helpers. Cost: the helpers never see my final sentences, so a badly worded sentence can reach you unchecked. That already happens today.
- **1b** (A, withdrawn) I keep writing it twice and paste the checked text unchanged, so the helpers check the exact words you read. Cost: the second writing stays, about 3% of my bill.

How to choose: pick 1a if simpler and cheaper matters more. Pick 1b if you want the helpers to check the exact sentences you read. I first picked 1b and changed my mind when C's measurement showed that this check is not real today.

Pitfalls avoided: me misquoting a helper in the final text is made less likely by the rule you already accepted, that I may only say a helper agreed where it wrote so (C). A summary so short that it hides a cost is prevented by requiring each option's reason, cost, evidence, risks and the step that removes each risk in it (B). Losing the record of what you decided is prevented because the summary file and your answer with its reasons stay in the chart (B,C). Whether the saving is real is checked by the later proof-of-saving question, which counts my writing per question (C).
