F1. I withdraw my systemctl-under-bus-cut concern: host-test-load/proofs.md:65–73 explicitly proves stop succeeds through the manager's private socket. Scope creation restores the bus only for adoptShell's subprocess (src/scope.ts:88), not run-check's workload (src/run-check.ts:56). A new privileged broker is not established as necessary; caller ownership validation remains required in the proposed command.
F2. “Only stopping the cgroup kills them all” overstates the evidence. The sweep proves cgroup stop worked after runner termination left browsers alive, not that no other cleanup can work. Also, 155 survivors were across two scopes, not one run (slots/sweep-results.md, final bullet; kill-B.md F6–F9).
F3. A stale sweep on the next run-check/allocate is eventual cleanup, with no bound when neither happens. It does not meet prompt owner-death cleanup and permits orphan resource use until another invocation. Keep independent lifetime supervision as my requirement; a scope plus such supervision is acceptable, a scope plus wrapper hooks alone is not (local systemd.scope(5), Description; kill-B.md F9/O3).
F4. `pkill --cgroup <pane-scope> -A` is narrower than host-wide matching but still targets sibling runs/workers in that pane, and -A only excludes ancestors. It is not “stop my run” and does not provide bounded escalation or verified emptiness (src/scope.ts:17/:71; local pgrep(1), --cgroup/--ignore-ancestors; kill-B.md F2/O2).
F5. Do not tag “workers signal nothing; only A stops runs” as my position. I proposed worker-owned handles and cancellation restricted to those handles, with explicit transfer on replacement (kill-B.md A2). A-only cancellation adds a coordination dependency without fixing owner-death cleanup.
F6. Keep cleanup on normal completion as well as cancellation. A server deliberately shared across separate checks needs an explicit longer-lived owner outside each check unit; abnormal-only cleanup leaves accidental normal-exit leaks intact. No inspected case yet establishes such a shared-server requirement (kill-B.md D3; merged Differ C is hypothetical).
