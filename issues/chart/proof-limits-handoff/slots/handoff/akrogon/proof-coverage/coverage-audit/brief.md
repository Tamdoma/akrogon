# Brief: coverage-audit

## What

Change the chart-issues door's written rules so a handoff checks that each promise's key part was actually observed before the leaf is handed off. Text only, in `skills/chart-issues/SKILL.md` ("Take", "Handoff"), `skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md` ("Preflight and validation"), `skills/chart-issues/assets/questions.md` ("Optional measurement") and `docs/use/plan-work.md` ("Review the handoff").

## Why

Tamdoma/akrogon#127: chart `about-titles` (framework) handed off leaf `fixture-weave-recorder` although its only proof's `limits` said verify acceptance, the property criterion 1 rests on, was not proven. The leaf then failed three times on that property, with three operator design stops, five grants and about ten paid sessions. Today the operation-proof rule is per external operation and the audit refuses only a missing proof; nothing compares a proof's `limits` with the criteria that depend on it. Worked example the new text must catch: framework `issues/closed/about-titles/fixture-weave-recorder/readiness.yaml` `proofs[0]` against `brief.md` criterion 1.

## Done-criteria

1. A reader of `skills/chart-issues/SKILL.md` "Take" learns one general rule that folds in the two special cases (provider dry-run, locked outside-tool setting), keeping their specifics: a proof counts only for the property it observed, which its `limits` records; and that every clause of a done-criterion whose pass rests on something existing before the leaf builds (an outside system's acceptance, an existing checker's verdict, existing data or recordings) is observed at charting by the smallest call that reaches it, free before paid, within the one operator-approved paid test budget; that a clause needing an existing checker to accept output the leaf builds is split, its existing input or contract assumption observed at charting and acceptance of the new output left to the leaf's own proof; and that every such observation, free ones included, is a `proofs` entry in `readiness.yaml` with its own `limits`.
2. A reader of "Take" learns that every operation and key part kept in the contract needs its proof before handoff; that a declined probe, or no safe one, cuts the contract to what is proven at the handoff review, naming what was cut and where it lands (a prerequisite leaf under the machinery rule, an exclusion in each dependent's design, or an Off route entry), and the cut removes that proof obligation and drops a cut operation from the brief's What; that a cut removing the chart's useful outcome parks the chart for the operator under the existing non-grant approval wait, never answered by A; that nothing unproven is handed off as promised ("no waiver" stays); and that "scope is not narrowed" no longer appears.
3. A reader of `skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md` "Preflight and validation" learns that the implementer audit, for each done-criterion, names the proof or charting observation that reached each pre-existing key part, and refuses the handoff when one rests only on a proof's `limits` or on no proof; the refusal of a missing proof stays.
4. A reader of `SKILL.md` "Handoff" learns that the handoff review carries one line per leaf listing its pre-existing key parts with the `proofs` entry that reached each and anything cut with its landing, information only like the live-run line, and that it states the change reaches doors only at the next release link when the chart changes the door's own skill.
5. `skills/chart-issues/assets/questions.md` "Optional measurement" and `docs/use/plan-work.md` "Review the handoff" agree with criteria 1 to 4: the measurement paragraph still never replaces required operation proof and its sentence "Declining a required probe holds the handoff under the Take operation-proof rule" is replaced by the cut rule, and the docs page names the new review line in one sentence.
6. `git --no-pager diff --name-only "$AKROGON_BASE"...HEAD` lists only the files named in What; `src/`, `tests/` and `issues/` are unchanged.
