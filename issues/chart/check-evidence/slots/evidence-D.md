### 3. Five parts for Q1

1. **The pick with its reason and cost**:
   - **Pick**: **In the repo, one compressed archive per closed owner (`issues/closed/<owner>/logs.tar.zst`, compressed with `zstd` when `completeOwner` moves `issues/open/<owner>` to `issues/closed/<owner>` at `src/phase.ts:224`), while keeping plain `.log` files in `issues/open/` while an owner is open or `failed`**.
   - **Reason**:
     - **Zero friction for active or `failed` leaves**: While any leaf in the issue/epic is still in `issues/open/` (including a leaf stopped in `phase: failed` on an incomplete base run under `base-red-rule.md:27`), all `.log` files remain plain uncompressed files at their exact paths (`<leaf>/implementation/check-*.log`), so seats (`implement-issue`, `check-issue`, `merge-issue`) and the operator can `Read`, `Grep`, and `tail` them directly.
     - **Cross-run deduplication shrinks `944.88 MiB` (`4,462` files) to `19–37 MiB` (`46` files) losslessly**: Because a single leaf/owner runs `akrogon verify` and `run-check` 5–15 times with 99% identical passing test output across runs, compressing all `.log` files of an owner together into one `logs.tar.zst` archive at owner completion achieves a **`25x–50x` size reduction** (`36.90 MiB` at `zstd -9`, `19.0 MiB` at `zstd -19`) and a **`99%` file-count reduction** (`4,460` files -> `45` archives in `framework/issues/closed`), whereas per-log `.log.zst` cannot deduplicate across runs of the same owner and leaves **`140.88 MiB` (`153.48 MiB` in 4 KiB disk blocks) across `4,462` files** in every checkout and `<slug>-u<N>` worker worktree.
     - **Self-contained across clones, worktrees, and backups**: Full lossless evidence travels with git history and `issues/closed/<owner>`, without creating a new `~1 GB` host store under `~/.akrogon`.
   - **Cost**:
     - Calling `tar --zstd -cf logs.tar.zst ...` (or `zstd -9`/`-19`) inside `completeOwner` (`src/phase.ts:221-225`, plus a helper for chart `direct` logs) before `renameSync(owner, destination)` and updating `failedTests` in `src/flaky.ts:10-17` to read `<rel>.log` from `issues/closed/<owner>/logs.tar.zst` when the plain `.log` path is not on disk (~30 lines of code).

2. **Each rejected option with its reason**:
   - **Rejected Option 1 — In the repo, compressed per-log (`<log>.zst`)**:
     - *Reason*: Compressing each `.log` file individually loses cross-run dictionary sharing across the 5–15 `check-verify-*.log` / `check-cheap-*.log` runs within the same issue/epic. On `framework`, per-log `zstd` still leaves **`140.88 MiB` (`153.48 MiB` in 4 KiB blocks) and `4,462` separate `.log.zst` files** in the working tree of the main checkout, every clone, and every `<slug>-u<N>` worker worktree (`worker-protocol.md:11`) — **`4x–8x` larger and `100x` more files** than one archive per closed owner (`19–37 MiB` in `46` files). And if done at `run-check` write time rather than `completeOwner`, it also breaks plain-text `Read`/`Grep`/`tail` for active seats in `issues/open/`.
   - **Rejected Option 2 — Outside the repo (`~/.akrogon/evidence` or `leafTemp`) with a short failure excerpt kept in the repo**:
     - *Reason*:
       1. A short excerpt in the repo is **lossy**, violating the seed's core requirement (`INTAKE.md:4, 25`: *"Check evidence kept losslessly"*) and breaking `src/flaky.ts:13` or post-mortem root-cause analysis whenever a failure's relevant lines sit outside the excerpt window.
       2. Putting the full logs in `leafTemp` and reaping them at leaf end breaks `issues/chart/test-runs/forks/base-red-rule.md:27` (*"keep logs"* on `failed` leaves, since `reconcile` removes a `failed` leaf's `leafTemp` once its tab closes) and destroys closed leaves' full logs.
       3. Putting the full logs in `~/.akrogon/evidence` moves `~1 GB` of unpruned logs into `~/.akrogon` (violating `worktree-cleanup`'s clean-`~/.akrogon` rule) and leaves every other `git clone` or repo backup without the full logs.

       - `akrogon`: raw `21.27 MiB` -> per-file `zstd -3` `5.91 MiB` (`8.33 MiB` 4K blocks, `819` files) vs per-owner `zstd -3` `2.06 MiB` / `zstd -9` `1.78 MiB` (`35` files).

4. **The pitfalls with what removes each**:
   - **Pitfall 1**: `completeOwner(repo, leaf, false)` can be called again on an already-closed or multi-leaf owner (`src/phase.ts:191, 780`, `src/next.ts:773`), or an epic can close private issue sources before the whole epic completes (`src/phase.ts:214-221`).
     - *What removes it*: Pack `logs.tar.zst` inside `completeOwner` only at the final `if (!complete) return;` gate (`src/phase.ts:221-225`) right before `renameSync(owner, destination)`, so it runs exactly once when the whole owner moves to `issues/closed/<owner>`.
   - **Pitfall 2**: `akrogon flaky` (`src/flaky.ts:10-17`) currently checks only `existsSync(recorded)` and `existsSync(recorded.replace('/issues/open/', '/issues/closed/'))` and returns `['(log gone)']` when the loose `.log` file has been packed into `logs.tar.zst`.
     - *What removes it*: Teach `failedTests(recorded)` in `src/flaky.ts:10-17` to fall back to extracting the relative path from `issues/closed/<owner>/logs.tar.zst` (`tar --zstd -xOf <archive> <relPath>`) when the loose file under `issues/closed/<owner>/` is absent.
   - **Pitfall 3**: Delegated worker worktrees `<slug>-u<N>` (`skills/implement-issue/worker-protocol.md:11`) use plain `git worktree add --detach` without sparse-checkout, checking out `issues/` and `learnings/` in every worker worktree even though workers are forbidden from touching `issues/` (`src/phase.ts:346-355`).
     - *What removes it*: Even with `issues/closed/` shrunk to `19–37 MiB`, archive at owner close keeps `<slug>-u<N>` worktrees small automatically without changing worker git commands.

5. **Any question the peer would ask that the fork does not ask**:
