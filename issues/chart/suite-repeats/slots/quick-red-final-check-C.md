# quick-red final check: seat C

D1. Item 1 makes known-red unreachable whenever a non-`full` red blocks the `full` check in the head pass, and the shape does not record this. A blocked entry is neither green evidence (`src/verify.ts:172-174` needs exit 0) nor red evidence (head and base blocked entries are skipped at `src/verify.ts:227` and `src/verify.ts:234`), so under the one-head-row judge a head row with a blocked `full` entry can never cover, no matter what the linked base pass shows. Today that limitation exists only for merge members (`src/verify.ts:567-575`), which akrogon does not use; item 1 extends it to the `checks` stage, where akrogon's known-red flow actually runs. Recoverable in akrogon today because its quick reds are almost always leaf-owned (fix format, rerun, test runs), and no current repo pairs a `full` declaration with `(fail)`-emitting quick checks that could be base-red. But as written the rule is general: for such a repo a base-red quick check blocks the `full` check in both head and base passes forever, and no seat route forces the `full` check to run. The plan should either record this limitation beside the member one or exempt known-red head and base passes from blocking. Q3 has no case covering known-red with a blocked `full` entry.
