## Q1: Should B run `akrogon verify` at the end of `check.repair` only when the move is `merge` (`src/phase.ts` `check.repair` to `merge` calls `requireGreenReceipt(..., 'leaf')`), and what about the done-criterion proof reruns in the same sentence?

### 1. Pick, reason, and cost
- **Pick**: Gate **both** parts of the closing sentence in `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` `## check.repair` (the paragraph beginning `"After repairs, B reruns each done-criterion proof..."`) on moving to `merge` (i.e., when nothing is handed to A and no operator action is open).
  - **During `check.repair`**: For each item B repairs itself, B continues to prove that repair immediately as already specified earlier in `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` `## check.repair` — a failing test commit proven red with a targeted `akrogon run-check` plus a fix commit proven green with a targeted `akrogon run-check` for behavior Fixes; before/after evidence (`akrogon run-check` or quoted text) for docs/command Fixes; and the planned live proof passing on the repaired head when a planned live proof was red.
  - **At the end of `check.repair`**:
    - **When moving to `merge`**: B reruns each done-criterion proof whose test, proof command, or inputs changed since its last green run on this leaf (in the `skills/implement-issue/SKILL.md` `## Shared context` proof order, skipping an unaffected full suite), and then runs `akrogon verify` on the committed head to write the `leaf` receipt that `src/phase.ts:431-432` requires on `check.repair` to `merge`.
    - **When moving to `check.fix`** (`Handed to A` non-empty): B runs neither the end-of-phase done-criterion proof rerun sweep nor `akrogon verify`. Instead, A's closing pass in `skills/implement-issue/SKILL.md` `## check.fix` runs once after A's repairs, covering all done-criterion proofs whose test, proof command, or inputs changed **since their last green run on the leaf** (which includes files touched by B's `check.repair` commits as well as A's `check.fix` commits) plus `akrogon verify` on the committed head before `check.fix` moves to `check.review` (`src/phase.ts:429`).
- **Reason**:
  1. **Gate alignment**: `src/phase.ts:427-432` requires a green `leaf` receipt on `requested === 'check.review'` (`src/phase.ts:429`) and on `state.phase === 'check.repair' && requested === 'merge'` (`src/phase.ts:431-432`). The move `check.repair` to `check.fix` has no receipt gate (`docs/reference/state-and-phases.md` `## Phases`; `docs/reference/checks.md` `## Receipt gates`). The prose in `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` `## check.repair` even states why `akrogon verify` is there — `"writes the receipt the move into merge requires"` (restored by `fb236ec0` on 2026-10-15) — yet omitted `"When moving to merge"`, causing B to run `akrogon verify` whenever B committed any repair before handing remaining items to `check.fix`.
  2. **Empirical waste of `akrogon verify` on `check.repair -> check.fix`**:
     - In `akrogon` since `fb236ec0` (`2026-10-15T12:00:00Z`), 38 `check.repair` visits exited to `check.fix`. In the 17 where B made no commits (`bChangedHead === false`), B skipped `akrogon verify` and moved straight to `check.fix`. In all **21 of 21** where B repaired at least one item and handed the rest to A (`bChangedHead === true`), B ran `akrogon verify` (**28 runs, 3,886s = 64.8 min wall**; since `2026-10-26`, **5 visits, 9 runs, 732s = 12.2 min wall** across `fewer-repeat-calls`, `one-test-run`, `verify-pass-service`, `durable-check-capture`, and `abandoned-capture-stop`). In **19 of 21** (90.5%, and **5 of 5 = 100%** since `2026-10-26`), A then committed new changes in `check.fix`, invalidating B's leaf receipt and running `akrogon verify` all over again before `check.review`. Even in the 2 older visits where A did not change `HEAD` in `check.fix`, `check.fix -> check.review` is itself gated by `requireGreenReceipt(..., 'leaf')` (`src/phase.ts:429`), so A guarantees the leaf receipt before `check.review`.
     - In `tamdoma/framework` since `2026-10-15T12:00:00Z`, 217 `check.repair` visits exited to `check.fix`: in **95 of 96** where B committed a partial repair (`bChangedHead === true`), B ran `akrogon verify` (**108 runs, 3,033s = 50.6 min wall**; since `2026-10-26`, **27 visits, 32 runs, 601s = 10.0 min wall**), and in **24 of 27 (88.9%)** since `2026-10-26` A then changed `HEAD` in `check.fix` and ran `akrogon verify` again.
  3. **Empirical waste of the end-of-`check.repair` done-criterion proof sweep on `check.repair -> check.fix`**:
     - When B moves to `merge`, `check.repair` is the last phase that checks done-criterion proofs (since `merge` runs only `akrogon verify` and `merge_checks`).
     - When B moves to `check.fix`, the leaf must pass through `check.fix` and then return to `check.review` (`src/routing.ts:95-110`). At the end of `check.fix`, `skills/implement-issue/SKILL.md` `## check.fix` requires A to rerun every done-criterion proof under the `## implement` rerun rule (`"whose test, proof command or inputs (the files it exercises and their helpers, fixtures, config and lockfiles) changed since its last green run on this leaf"`).
     - Running that done-criterion sweep at the end of `check.repair` before handing to `check.fix` duplicates the heaviest E2E/browser/chain proofs on an intermediate head that A is about to modify. In `tamdoma/framework` since `2026-10-26`, across the 27 `check.repair -> check.fix` visits where B made a commit, B spent **3,917s (65.3 min)** of `check_end` time outside `akrogon verify` — e.g., in `all-kind-design-review` (`2026-10-27`) B repaired `F1` in 256ms (`repair-b-f1-red` 128ms, `repair-b-f1-green` 128ms) and then spent **808s (13.5 min)** running `repair-b-audit-fixtures` (68.1s) and `repair-b-chain-full` (740.1s) before handing off to `check.fix`, where A modified `HEAD` and had to rerun them again; in `launcher-ready` (`2026-10-27`) B spent **995s (16.6 min)** of check time across `repair-b-e2e-kit`, `repair-b-kit-local-dev`, `repair-b-client-acceptance`, `repair-b-e2e-conv`, `repair-b-fill-local`, `repair-b-crawl-compare`, `repair-b-end-offer`, and `repair-b-route-fidelity` right before handing to `check.fix`.
- **Cost**: Zero code changes (0 lines added or touched in `src/`). A one-sentence edit in `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` `## check.repair` (qualifying `"After repairs, B reruns..."` with `"When moving to merge after repairs, B reruns..."` and noting that on a move to `check.fix`, A's `check.fix` closing pass covers done-criterion proof reruns — including inputs touched by B's repair commits — and `akrogon verify`). If B's repair in `check.repair` broke an unrelated test or done-criterion proof on a leaf moving to `check.fix`, that regression is caught at the end of `check.fix` (minutes later, on the combined repair head) rather than before the handoff to A, while B's own repaired Fix is still proven red-then-green by B's targeted `akrogon run-check` in `check.repair`.

### 2. Rejected options and reasons
- **Keep `akrogon verify` at the end of all `check.repair` visits (status quo)**: Rejected. `src/phase.ts:431-432` gates only `check.repair -> merge`; running `akrogon verify` on `check.repair -> check.fix` burned 28 runs (64.8 min) in `akrogon` and 108 runs (50.6 min) in `tamdoma/framework` since `2026-10-15`, with 90.5% (100% since `2026-10-26` in `akrogon`, 88.9% in `tamdoma/framework`) immediately invalidated by A's `check.fix` commits.
