Then move substantial phase and mode procedures out of the multi-mode entry files. Keep their selection conditions in the entry files. Do not build a prompt compiler, generated copies or a section-extraction service.

W7 precedes W8. W5 removes the blanket requirement to read inactive references, while preserving fresh phase reads and reads at delegation, including delegated repair. Keep all second-seat reviews, clean-checkout checks, approvals, records and chart-door exchanges. Change no question-round rules. Prose caps and post-compaction partial rereads remain off route. No earlier decision needs reopening on the evidence below.

The strongest uncertainty is installation and activation, especially agy. The command sends a skill name and phase, not its text. A smaller section is useful only if the harness actually reads that section and can reach its common rules. File-size reductions are measurable now. Dollar, latency and behavioral improvements are not established by those counts.

## 3. Material operator forks

### Q1. How wide is the shared policy extraction?

- **Q1a, pick:** centralize the four requested families and their necessary bindings. Retain phase restrictions and differences explicitly, including review's no-covered-operation rule, merge-check template exception, standalone/direct output behavior and record targets. Keep existing lesson/standing-design authorities. Other families in F5 are an inventory and extraction constraints, not permission for a second policy rewrite. One shared file, no generator. Cost today: E1. Design basis: S1.
- **Q1b:** centralize every similar family, including retry, tests, blockers and proof order. It may remove more copies, but also broadens privileges, joins unrelated phase semantics and makes every phase read rules for operations it cannot perform. F5 shows why this is not mechanical deduplication. The extra savings have **no number** isolated beyond the counts reported in E1; no additional win is charted on that assumption.
- **Q1c:** harmonize differing behaviors first, then extract. This would require separate decisions on template access, cleanup distribution and producer/read permission. It can settle ambiguity but can also reopen approval and proof contracts accidentally. Pick Q1a because “no rule lost” is best reviewed when the change preserves behavior. Any requested semantic correction gets its own explicit question and criterion, not a quiet union of permissions.

Do not treat C's setup sentence as part of grants, or add the cleanup clause to every phase merely because it was appended to two grant paragraphs. Preserve current effective authority and record known discrepancies as such.

### Q2. What mechanism makes a phase or mode read smaller?

- **Q2a:** keep whole entry files and instruct named-section reads. Fewest files, compatible with the existing consultant reading rule, but full-body activation can defeat initial savings. It also leaves fragile imports into unread sections. Cost today: E2/E3. S2–S5 explain the limit.
- **Q2b, pick:** small entry files for multi-mode skills, direct links to the common policy and substantial phase/mode files. Name the current phase's input/procedure closure explicitly. Move only cross-phase definitions needed by that closure to a shared local section/file. Keep single-phase P/M together where no useless read remains. No parser or generated prompt. E2/E3 quantify the exposed text; S2–S4 support conditional supporting files. This adds real files but removes dependence on every harness slicing a monolithic activation correctly.
- **Q2c:** have akrogon assemble or inject selected bodies. It can enforce delivery more directly, but now owns a second dispatcher, ordering, manual-call parity, path/release handling and new failure behavior. No measured need for that machinery. E4 shows dispatch does not currently assemble text; S1/S2 favor one authoritative representation and shallow references.

Under any choice, resolve agy discovery before promising four-harness behavior. First establish what the installed 1.5.4 actually discovers, with a read-only local inventory or inspected implementation. If it cannot find the entry, the smaller primary change is an explicit canonical entry path in the ordinary phase task, rather than installing more guessed global roots. That is a concrete decision for Q2's final shape, not an unapproved fallback in the leaf. Current evidence E4 and S5 is insufficient to choose a new agy root. No agy config edit is authorized by this map.

### Q3. What result is sufficient to claim “no rule lost” and a saving?

- **Q3a, pick:** thinking-agent semantic audit of every old obligation against the selected new read closures, mechanical file/reference/install checks, and fixture delivery/transition checks where changed. Measure required text before/after, explicitly label it as exposure rather than dollars or behavior. Retain B's review. Cheapest sufficient proof for a text-organization contract, with limits stated.
