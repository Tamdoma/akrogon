## Q4. Smallest changes to restore useful throughput

**D1. Remove the hard 48 GiB start veto, without restoring the old whole-run permit.** That directly removes the demonstrated state where every admitted check is gone yet every new check is blocked. Keep wrapper evidence and ownership. Keeping 30-second start spacing initially is a smaller change than rewriting all scheduling, and no record here makes it the cause of multi-hour zero-start periods. This is a recommendation, not an executed change or a promise that uncontrolled expensive tests will become fast. Basis: F1, C2–C3 and the multi-hour waits in Q2.

**D2. Move bulky scratch off tmpfs while retaining short socket paths and run-scoped cleanup.** Do not simply revert to the old long TMPDIR that broke Chrome. Choose a genuinely disk-backed short scratch root and apply it to the producers that bypass leafTemp as well, particularly `/tmp/swi` and page-measure's length fallback. Reclaim existing scratch only after establishing that its owning work ended. A root-path change alone leaves today's files and the two hard-coded `/tmp` producers untouched. Basis: C1 and `FW/.claude/hooks/tests/scratch-writer-isolation.test.ts:71–78`, `page-measure.mjs:359–395`, `AK/src/run-check.ts:199–200`, `src/reconcile.ts:411–443`.

**D3. Restore the session bus to seats, keeping check-specific isolation if needed.** Do not require credential rotation or one-off command prefixes to compensate for a deliberately broken seat environment. Preserve per-pane scopes and process attribution. Favicon demonstrates why the narrow direct-git/gh exception is insufficient. Basis: C5 and favicon review `:373–393`.

Those are the smallest durable corrections supported here. For immediate recovery, D1 plus restoring affected seats' bus access removes the demonstrated start/auth blockades. D2 removes the retained-storage pressure feeding the problem. The memory drop observed at 07:27 shows why current recovery decisions should use fresh counters rather than assume the gate is still shut.

Keep the framework test-cost leaves and resolve their actual findings: bounded dependency selection, parity launch-budget proof and complete configured verification remain necessary (L4–L6). Do not substitute higher timeouts, repeated bare suites or reclassified incomplete runs for green evidence. The CPU quota is already gone. There is insufficient window-specific evidence to make removal of MemoryHigh, CPUWeight or scopes part of the minimum fix, and no measured basis for promising a particular October 12 merge rate (F5, C4).

1. **A1:** “30–42 GiB of leftovers” is too strong. Shared memory was large, but its exact useful/unused split and overnight history are not established. Leaf scratch was only one of several tmpfs locations (F2–F4).
