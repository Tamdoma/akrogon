- **R1. Reject K2 and A's reference-scan lean (`temp-gc-merged.md:13,16`).** Absence from cwd, open descriptors, argv and environment does not prove a file is unused. The wrapper keeps capture paths in its JavaScript heap and opens them after the child exits (`src/shell.ts:34–35,54–61`). Between child exit and that read, the files need not appear in any listed reference. Two snapshots cannot close the scan-to-delete race. Conversely, treating a seat's inherited `TMPDIR=<leaf-root>` as a reference to every descendant protects every leftover while that seat lives (`src/next.ts:478`). Thus the scan either misses future users or retains the whole tree. Keep explicit ownership through evidence capture, then reclaim after retirement and descendant shutdown. This is the distinction in `temp-gc-B.md:24–28`, not simply a preference for more machinery.
