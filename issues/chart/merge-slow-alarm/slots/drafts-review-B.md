# Slot B draft disagreements

- **DR1. Episode ordering is unspecified for concurrent passes.** The design carries “earlier,” “first flagged,” “last 20” and “inside the episode” without choosing start order, completion order or receipt-lock finalization order, or naming that choice as open for the plan. These can differ: `src/verify.ts:runOnce` appends a receipt before checkout, assigns `end` before acquiring the final rewrite lock, and updates the existing row without reordering the ledger. Two overlapping passes can therefore produce different origins, reference cohorts and episode closes depending on the reader's ordering. Specify the ordering shared by the writer and status reader, including a stable tie rule where needed, or explicitly delegate that decision to the plan. **Evidence:** draft `design.md`, Binding decisions Q1–Q3 and Leaf architecture; `src/verify.ts:runOnce`; `shapes.md`, Leaf files, requires each changed state's consumer and its decision or an `Open for the plan:` question.

- **DR2. The proof interpretation does not meet the outside-stage recording rule as written.** The design says the criteria use the existing `FAKE_HERDR`, but `tests/fake-herdr.ts` hand-constructs its notification success response and simulated failures. `readiness.yaml` records a real call, but the design does not say that the real consumers will replay its recorded output or name a preserved recording for that proof. A separate successful CLI call establishes operation feasibility, not recorded-output replay through the changed consumer. Specify a compliant proof path using the actual recording or the smallest permitted fresh interaction. If replay requires new or extended proof machinery, name the prerequisite rather than leaving it to this product leaf. **Evidence:** draft `design.md`, standing-design interpretation; draft `readiness.yaml`, proofs; `tests/fake-herdr.ts`, notification-show handler; `standing-design.md`, outside-stage recorded-output rule and prohibition on a product leaf building or extending its own proof machinery.
