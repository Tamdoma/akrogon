# Final check, slot C

F1. Wrong fact in twin-guard design, Leaf architecture, and it makes criterion 1 unmeetable as specified there: leaves in a `next --all` pass are not "unpicked". `sweepAll` and the current-repo `--all` sweep in `src/next.ts` both call `sweep(..., true, isAutomatic)`, and `sweep` forwards that `true` as `picked` into every `dispatchLeaf` call; `picked=false` occurs only on the `--resume`/automatic sweep, the hook paths and `dispatchMergeLeaf`. So the design's two settled bullets contradict each other for `--all`: "an explicitly picked twin leaf throws the refusal like a picked leaf with missing inputs" and "unpicked twin leaves in `next --all` are reported once per owner through `report` keyed by the open owner folder". Followed literally, every twin leaf in `--all` is picked, throws, and lands in `dispatchLeaf`'s catch, which reports keyed by `leaf.path` and slug, one JSON line per leaf, not the "prints that error once for the owner" criterion 1 requires. As implementer I cannot satisfy both bullets and criterion 1. The shape that meets the criteria is one non-throwing twin branch for both forms: report once keyed by the open owner folder (which sets exit 1 through `invocation.skipped`, satisfying the slug form's non-zero exit) and return without dispatching. The design should state that and drop the picked/unpicked split, or change criterion 1.

F2. The twin block as placed never reaches two phases, against the brief's What ("in every phase") and the design's "every phase is refused, including `merge`". In `dispatchLeaf` (`src/next.ts`) the `merged` branch calls `completeOwner` and the `failed` branch returns or throws its recovery error, both before `blockDetail` is computed. A merged twin leaf therefore still runs the completion retry and fails with `Completion destination exists: .../issues/closed/<name>`, reported per leaf from the catch, not with the twin refusal naming both folders once per owner; a failed twin leaf gets no twin refusal at all. Neither is dispatched, so the safety property holds, but the error contract in the What and criterion 1 does not, and a `--all` pass over the real twin shape (merged leaves, swept first by `sweepAll`) prints per-leaf destination errors before any twin line. The design also does not state the twin check's precedence inside `blockDetail` and `eligibility` relative to their existing deps and inputs checks, which decides which error a picked twin leaf with unmerged deps or missing inputs prints. State explicitly: either the twin check runs in `dispatchLeaf` before the `merged`/`failed` branches (and first inside both block functions), or merged and failed twin leaves are carved out of the What and the destination throw remains their error.
