# Name candidates A, round 3

New names (whole-word grep over skills/ and src/, *.md and *.ts, 2026-11-04: 0 hits each):

1. **basecamp**. Step: "set up basecamp"; record: "the basecamp" (`basecamp.md`). An expedition sets up one basecamp before it enters the territory: it settles what the trip is for, what matters most, the hard limits, and who decides at which forks; every route into the territory starts from it and returns to it. That is one step per session covering several charts, which no other candidate says. Cost: names the place and moment more than the content; "basecamp" is one word in hiking usage but often written "base camp".

2. **vantage**. Step: "take the vantage" is out ("take" is the fork word), so "climb to the vantage"; record "the vantage". The high point you climb first to see the whole territory before drawing any map, echoing "akro" (top). Cost: says seeing, not choosing; weak on votes.

3. **muster**. Step: "the muster"; record `muster.md`. The gathering before setting out where the party agrees its aims and roles. Cost: not a land-map word; leans military.

## Top 3 across the whole list

1. **basecamp**: the only name that says "once, before the territory, for the whole expedition", which is the operator's per-session rule.
2. **signposts**: names both halves (where to steer, which forks need the operator) at the forks themselves.
3. **stakes**: both halves in one word, but "stakes" also reads as "risk".
