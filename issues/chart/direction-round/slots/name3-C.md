# Name candidates C, round 3

New names, not on the list. Whole-word grep over skills/ and src/ (`*.md`, `*.ts`), 2026-11-04: 0 hits each.

1. **survey**. Step: "run the survey"; record: "the survey" (`survey.md`). Before any map is drawn the surveyor fixes the datum, the scale and what the map is for; every later line is measured against it. One survey per session, before any map, that all charts inherit is exactly the surveyor's job. Cost: the word also means a questionnaire, which is true of the round but makes it sound like a poll; it says little about which forks stay the operator's.

2. **charter**. Step: "write the charter"; record: "the charter" (`charter.md`). The written mandate an expedition sets out under: its purpose, priorities, limits, and what the leader keeps for themself. It sits one letter from "chart", so it reads as the thing charts are made under. Cost: more legal than overland, and a session is short for a word that usually means a standing document.

3. **blazes**. Step: "cut the blazes"; record: "the blazes" (`blazes.md`); a fork the operator keeps is "blazed". Blazes are the marks cut on trees at forks by whoever walked first, so those behind keep the route without a map; cutting them once and following them everywhere is the per-session rule. Cost: "blaze" also means fire, and it is better at the votes half than the priorities half.

## Top 3 across the whole list

1. **stakes**: the only one-word name that says both halves plainly, priorities ("at stake") and kept forks ("staked"), in the surveying act that precedes a map.
2. **signposts**: says both halves at the forks themselves and needs no explanation, at the cost of two syllables more and a hint that the route is posted.
3. **basecamp**: the best word for "once per expedition, before the territory", weaker on what the record holds.
