# Land-vocabulary names B

Read: `issues/closed/charting-vocabulary/rename-vocabulary/brief.md`, `README.md`, `docs/start/what-it-is.md`. The established vocabulary describes territory and choices along a route. The operator retains product decisions. These names fit that vocabulary without implying the route is already settled.

- **O1 · Signposts.** “Set the signposts” / “the signposts.” States what should guide choices at forks and which forks require the operator's vote. Fits the session-wide record: priorities and reserved votes stay available across every chart. Its cost is that the name needs a short definition to distinguish it from ordinary hints.
- **O2 · Waymarks.** “Set the waymarks” / “the waymarks.” Names durable marks that guide the work through the territory, including places where A must stop for the operator's choice. Compact and consistent with map, fork and route. Less familiar than signposts.
- **O3 · Trailhead.** “Start at the trailhead” / “the trailhead.” Gives the session a clear starting step before mapping: establish priorities and who chooses which forks. Familiar land vocabulary, but emphasizes the opening more than the continuing authority of the record.

Collision check: `rg -n -i -w -o 'signposts?|waymarks?|trailheads?' skills src` returned no matches on 2026-11-04. Singular and plural forms were checked.

**Favorite: signposts.** It best covers both steering priorities and marking the forks reserved for the operator.
