# Brief: signposts-step

## What

The chart door (`skills/chart-issues`) gains the signposts step: one round per door session, at Open before any map, where the session's general direction is set (ranked priorities, hard limits, order or drop of the session's charts) and then steers every map and every later pick. Three modes decide who answers: manual (the operator answers the signposts and every fork), autopilot (the operator answers the signposts once, A answers every fork), full autopilot, "autopilot signposts" (A answers both). The docs that describe the door's order and its modes say the same.

## Why

Tamdoma/akrogon#128 and the operator's words in issues/chart/direction-round/INTAKE.md: "to have a procedure that allows me (or you on autopilot) to pick the priorities and steer the whole process in the right direction and to get important impact and steering votes", one round "per entire session across all charts". Today the door goes from the map straight to single forks, and autopilot picks rest on a fixed generic value list.
