## Pitfalls and removal

- R1: A green disposable run certifies the wrong SHA, or setup mutates tracked files. Bind candidate/base/commands/environment to the proof and verify the exact pushed candidate, including top, solo and restack/reuse paths.
- R2: A fresh checkout placed inside a repo is discovered by a scanner, creating a new race. Declare scratch location outside scanned source and clean only owned disposable resources, including children on failure.
- R3: A pool releases while descendants still consume capacity, or nested acquisition deadlocks. Define lifetime around the whole process tree and one acquisition boundary. Waiting is not a defect bounce or repair attempt.
- R4: Serializing whole suites leaves internal lane races alive. Fix fixture ownership at the writer/scanner boundary. Preserve real failure status rather than retrying until green.
- R5: New work repeats already-owned throughput policy. Reuse red-main-hold, bounce-repair-proof, red-batch-culprit and merge-attempt-records rather than changing cause routing, repair caps or attempt schemas again. Batch-limit controls stack size, not host test capacity. Keep framework suite selection/release ownership in its existing framework-test-scope chart.
