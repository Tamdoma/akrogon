## Where slots differ — my positions

### 1. Review at `fix_rounds` 0: I withdraw my earlier line and join (A,C) — `requiredSlots` returns `['B']` for a direct leaf

My merged line (genuine blind A review) was wrong on scope, and I am correcting it. The fork's Carries bound #153 to "tracking, status and broadcast only"; the direct route's review discipline — door implements, B alone reviews — is not in scope to change. A mandatory A self-review adds ceremony no operator answer asked for, on every direct attempt, forever. The operator lock's "exactly like a lifecycle leaf" names what status *shows* (phase, seats, runs), not the verdict composition; a missing A verdict is an honest absence, and B-only review is already a lifecycle-sanctioned shape — `requiredSlots(check.review, rounds > 0)` returns `['B']` in the unmodified lifecycle (`src/routing.ts`, read 2026-11-25).

What each other choice breaks:

- **My earlier line (genuine blind A review):** scope — it rewrites the direct route's review discipline inside an issue about visibility. It also costs a full review pass whose independence is weaker than it looks, because the same continuous door session implements and then reviews.
- **D's line (record A's verdict from the door's proof runs):** the record lies. `verdict.A` in a leaf record means a check.review pass with its duties happened (`skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` `## check.review`: diff judged against plan/brief/design, AREA.md existence checks, test comparison — none of which is the implement-end proof pass). Recording the verdict without the pass makes the operator-visible record false in the exact field this fork exists to make trustworthy. The honest version of D's insight is A/C: record nothing for A, because nothing is owed.

### 2. Repair after a `fix` verdict: C's line, with the merge-exit corollary nobody has stated

Position: for a direct leaf, a `fix` verdict routes to `check.fix` (the door repairs as A), matching the direct route's existing discipline — direct-route.md "Repairs B's fixes and asks B to check again" is the door repairing. This is also mechanically self-consistent in a way the merged notes did not spell out: `check.fix` increments `fix_rounds` (`src/phase.ts:commitMove`), so every re-review after the first repair falls under the existing `rounds > 0` rule and is B-only *without any conditional in requiredSlots* — the only conditional needed is the initial-review one from item 1.

What D's line breaks: the door cannot honestly hop `check.repair -> check.fix`. `src/phase.ts:transition` fills a missing `--slot` with the single required slot, and `check.repair` requires B (`src/routing.ts`) — so the hop either refuses or is recorded as B's move with B in `done`, a false attribution in the record this fork exists to make true. It is also two moves where one routing conditional suffices.

The unstated corollary: review-verdict repairs and merge-exit repairs are different inputs and the fork must decide both. Land and the merge turn hardcode ejection to `check.repair` (`src/land.ts:repairExit`, `src/next.ts:mergeTurnExit` — read 2026-11-25), and `commitMove` writes the `merge_exit` evidence only when moving to `check.repair`. If direct leaves route review-fixes to `check.fix` but merge exits still land in `check.repair`, then either B repairs merge exits (lifecycle-exact, and arguably right — red judgment is check-issue's check.repair expertise, and this capability is new to the direct route, so nothing is being changed) or the exit destination needs its own direct-leaf conditional plus a `merge_exit` recording adjustment. Silence here strands the leaf in a phase whose required slot the direct discipline never staffs.

### 4. The door's wait: D's line, and it is not optional — the repair loop has no other actuator

The command prompts nobody for a direct leaf, so if the leaf ejects to `check.fix`/`check.repair` after a red or conflict, nothing anywhere starts the repair — the leaf sits forever unless the door is present. The in-turn watch is the minimal form of the only mechanism that can close that loop. C's wake (a command→door send on merge resolution) is a new mechanism that should exist only if real queue waits prove long; it duplicates what the door's presence already must accomplish. Two more things the same watch closes for free: the chart-side bookkeeping the merged notes dropped — `akrogon pack` and the `Closed <date>` marker are the surviving tail of direct-route.md step 9 and still need the door after the machine lands — and the broadcast send, which happens minutes after the merge while B is still warm. ## Points missing from the merged notes

- **M1 — busy and blocked notices leak across charts (wrongly absent from the pitfall list).** The busy read the merged notes want (`observeBusy` by recorded address) does more than display: on a `blocked` status it writes an operator notice containing the last 40 screen lines, and past one hour `working` it writes a stall notice (`src/next.ts:observeBusy` — read 2026-11-25). For a borrowed door terminal, both fire on unrelated charting work: the operator gets "Blocked leaf" notices with screen dumps of a *different chart's* content, and stall notices for a leaf that is simply waiting its turn while the door works elsewhere. C's scoping to required slots reduces but does not remove this — during `implement` the door is always busy with *something*. What removes it: for direct leaves, `observeBusy` records `busy_since` for display but skips the notice writes — one conditional at the two `writeNotice` call sites.
- **M2 — the frozen-record gap can be closed at creation instead of named.** The merged notes accept that `moveGuards`' frozen-citation gate never runs for a leaf starting at `implement` and that this merely needs naming. Worse than stated: `requireTestChangeCitations` covers only M/D/T test-file changes, so without a frozen record *added* test files escape citation entirely (`src/phase.ts` — read 2026-11-25). But the gap is removable for free: the creation verb writes the frozen record as it stands at creation — an empty object, since the branch is at its base — and `state.frozen !== undefined` makes the gate run with exactly the right semantics (every added test file needs a `Test-Change:` trailer, nothing else changes). In lifecycle the record is written at the `plan.synthesis -> implement` move (`frozenRecord`), when the branch is likewise unmodified — an empty record at direct-leaf creation is the exact analog.
- **M3 — merge-exit routing for direct leaves is undecided** (the corollary under item 2): who repairs a red/conflict/gate ejection, and whether `merge_exit`'s recording survives the routing chosen. This is not the same question as review-fix repair and the merged notes treat neither half.

In particular `endFinishedSeats` runs for a merged leaf whose tab is not live, which is always for a tab-less direct leaf: it is the single catastrophic kill path and it is live by default. (Evidence: `src/reconcile.ts` `endFinishedSeats`/`closeMergedTab`/`closeTabOf`, `src/next.ts:renameTabs`, `src/status.ts:unnamedTabs` — read 2026-11-25.)
