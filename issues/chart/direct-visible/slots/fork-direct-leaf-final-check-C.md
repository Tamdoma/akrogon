# Final-shape check: slot C — direct-visible / direct-leaf

Defects and conflicts found in `fork-direct-leaf-final-shape.md` (items marked `[new]` and item 4).

## Item 2 `[new]` (direct verb)

- **Rerun on a merged/closed leaf conflicts with Item 17**: Once `src/reconcile.ts` `removeMergedWorktree` sweeps a merged direct leaf's worktree and branch, `state.worktree` is `undefined`. If the verb detects first-run vs rerun by `state.worktree === undefined`, rerunning it per Item 17 on a closed `broadcast pending` leaf would try to recreate a worktree/branch on a merged leaf. *Fix*: treat any `merged`/`failed` leaf (or any leaf with `seat.A`/`seat.B` already recorded) as seat-rewrite-only — never touch worktrees or `.env` on rerun or terminal phases.
- **Session validation vs `broadcastInProgress`**: `HC_TERMINAL_ID` carries only an address, not a session, while `src/reconcile.ts` `broadcastInProgress` reads `repoTerminals(repo)` (only `repoSpace(repo).session`). The verb must resolve the session through `src/hc.ts` `repoSpace` (matching `src/akrogon.ts` `seat --slot`) and validate that both `seat.A` and `seat.B` are `state === 'live'` in that session, or `broadcastInProgress` will fail to see `seat.B`.
- **Missing `checkBase` and `ensureLeafTemp` on first run**: `src/next.ts` `dispatchLeaf` + `allocate` run `src/preflight.ts` `checkBase(repo, mustCreate)` and `src/config.ts` `ensureLeafTemp` before `createSparseWorktree(..., trackingRef(repo), ...)`. Skipping `checkBase` lets first-run creation branch from a missing or stale tracking ref.
- **Stale `delivery_error.B` on rerun (interacts with Items 16 & 17)**: If the prior `seat.B` failed broadcast send (`delivery_error.B` recorded) and Item 17 rewrites `seat.B` to a new live terminal, "rewrites `seat.A` and `seat.B` and nothing else" leaves the old terminal's `delivery_error.B` in state. Both rewriting `seat.B` to a different address and a started send in `broadcastPass` (Item 16) must clear `delivery_error.B` (never `prompted`), or `src/status.ts` `note` and `leafAttention` will keep showing `B prompt not_started` and `red: true`.

## Item 4 (command prohibitions on a direct leaf)

- **`akrogon stop --all` misses active direct leaves**: `src/stop.ts` `stopCommand` filters `(leaf.state.tab !== undefined || leaf.state.batch !== undefined) && leaf.state.phase !== 'merged'`. Because Item 3 leaves `state.tab` undefined, `stop --all` skips active direct leaves outside `merge` (`implement`, `check.review`, `check.repair`, `check.fix`), conflicting with "slice stop and run cancellation stay as for any leaf". *Fix*: include `leaf.state.direct === true && leaf.state.worktree !== undefined` in `stopCommand --all` (with `stopLeaf` skipping `endSeats`).
- **Tab-rename and `unnamedTabs` guards omitted**: Item 4 lists start/prompt/kill/count paths but omits explicit `direct` guards in `src/next.ts` `renameTabs`, `src/phase.ts` `renameTab` (called from `announceFailed` and `commitMove`), and `src/status.ts` `unnamedTabs`, relying only on `tab === undefined`. Add explicit `direct` guards for the same defense-in-depth Item 4 requires on kill/close paths.
- **`reconcile` wipes `leafTemp` (`AKROGON_EVIDENCE`) mid-attempt**: In `src/reconcile.ts` `reconcile` (`scratchRoot` loop), a leaf with `tab === undefined` and no active process in `leafSlice` has its `leafTemp` dropped by `dropChecked(dir)` even while `!finished(leaf)`. Since direct seats run outside `leafSlice`, any reconcile pass between a `run-check` and review deletes `leafTemp/evidence/` (`src/run-check.ts` `checkedRun`). *Fix*: keep `leafTemp` for direct leaves while `!finished(leaf)`.
- **`dispatchLeaf` short-circuit placement**: The early stop in `src/next.ts` `dispatchLeaf` must run *after* `observeBusy` and *after* `state.phase === 'merged'` -> `completeOwner` (so a transient `closeSources` failure in `land` still retries owner completion on `next`), and before `allocate`/`dispatchSlot`.

## Item 5 `[new]` (busy observation)

- **Stale `busy_since` survives phase transitions**: `src/phase.ts` `commitMove` preserves `recorded.busy_since` on all non-terminal moves (`busy_since: to === 'failed' || to === 'merged' ? {} : recorded.busy_since`). If `observeBusy` only reads/writes `busy_since` for the current phase's `requiredSlots`, then `busy_since.A` recorded in `implement` stays stuck in `state.yaml` throughout `check.review` (`['B']`) and `merge` (`[]`), showing bogus `busy A <elapsed>` in `src/status.ts` `note` and `leafJson`. *Fix*: clear `busy_since` for slots outside `requiredSlots(state)` (in `dispatchLeaf` and/or `commitMove` for direct leaves).

## Item 6 `[new]` (TIME without `prompt` timing rows)

- **`src/timing-report.ts` flags every direct visit incomplete**: `visitGaps` requires a delivered `prompt` timing row for every slot in `historicalSlots(visit.phase, visit.rounds)` on non-`seatless` visits, and `promptedTotal` returns `0` without one (reporting 100% of active work as `wait none`). Additionally, `visitGaps` hardcodes `visit.phase === 'check.review' && visit.rounds === 0` to require a `seat_done` event, which `src/phase.ts` `transition` only emits when `required.length > 1`. *Fix*: make `visitGaps` aware of direct visits (skip missing-`prompt` flags for door-driven visits, and gate `seat_done` on `slots.length > 1`).
- **`status --json` parity**: `src/status.ts` `leafJson` (`first_prompt`) and `statusCommand` (`merges[].total_ms`) both read `starts(timings)`. The direct start-time source must feed `starts` (or all three callers), not only the text `TIME` cell.

## Item 7 `[new]` (Seats display)

- **`status --json` `schema: 1` contract and no-`hc` invariant**: `src/status.ts` `JsonLeaf` defines `seats.{a,b}` as `{ harness: string; command: string; source: string }` (from `src/config.ts` `seats`), and `status --json` makes zero `hc` calls. `state.seat.{A,B}` stores only `{ session, address }`. Replacing `JsonLeaf.seats` with `{ session, address }` breaks `schema: 1` consumers, while calling `hc` inside `jsonRepo` breaks `status --json`'s no-`hc` design. *Fix*: distinguish the surfaces — in `status <slug>`, replace "Seats for the next agent start" with the recorded `state.seat` terminals; in `status --json`, preserve `JsonLeaf.seats` compatibility (e.g. add optional recorded-terminal fields without calling `hc`).

## Item 9 `[new]` (Slots function taking `State`)

- **`src/timing-report.ts` `visitGaps` has no `State` object**: `visitGaps` calls `src/routing.ts` `historicalSlots(visit.phase, visit.rounds)` using `Visit` built from `log.jsonl`/`timings.jsonl`. Updating `requiredSlots` to take `State` does not reach `historicalSlots` unless `timing-report.ts` also passes the leaf's direct flag (from `repoInventory(repo, ['open', 'closed'])`) or records/derives it.

## Item 10 `[new]` (Flow: lifecycle `check-issue` for B and `check.repair` as A)

- **Consultant B's `cwd` is `repo.root`, breaking lifecycle `check-issue` assumptions**: `src/seat.ts` `launch` starts chart consultants in `repo.root` (with no `AKROGON_BASE` and no `TMPDIR=<leafTemp>`). Sending B the plain lifecycle `check-issue <slug> slot=B phase=check.review leaf=<folder>` prompt breaks three things in `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md` `## Shared context`:
  1. `akrogon config` run from `repo.root` (`src/config.ts` `effectiveConfig`) sees `top === repo.root`, omitting `AKROGON_BASE` and failing to resolve `leafPath`.
  2. `akrogon run-check` run from `repo.root` (`src/run-check.ts` `leafTarget`) throws `No leaf worktree matches cwd; pass --leaf or --holder`.
  3. Lifecycle `check-issue` does not instruct B to send `hc send <door> "done B: ..."`, which the door waits on under `questions.md` `## Blind peer exchange`.
  *Fix*: pass `worktree=<path>` (or have `check-issue` resolve `worktree` from `state.yaml` and run `akrogon config`/`run-check`/`git` inside it) and include the `hc send <door>` completion report on direct reviews.
- **Unreviewed self-merge hole in `check.repair -> merge` for slot A**: Item 10 relies on prose ("unedited return to `merge`, or `check.fix` for edits"), but `src/phase.ts` `transition` does NOT enforce unedited return — it permits any edited `check.repair -> merge` that holds a `leaf` receipt (safe only when reviewer B owns `check.repair`). Giving `check.repair` to author A (Item 9) lets A resolve a conflict or fix a red test in `check.repair` and move straight to `merge` without B's review. *Fix*: in `src/phase.ts` `transition`, for `state.direct === true && state.phase === 'check.repair' && requested === 'merge'`, refuse unless `state.merge_exit?.reason === 'red' && head === state.merge_exit.top`.

## Item 11 `[new]` (Door stays in turn until leaf leaves `merge`)

- **No non-polling wait primitive for queued/retried merges**: `src/next.ts` `mergeTurn` launches `akrogon-land-<attempt>` detached, no unit exists while waiting behind another leaf in `src/turn.ts` `mergeQueue`, and `src/land.ts` `keepPlace` clears `batch` and spawns a new `attempt` on a second non-fast-forward refusal. Staying in-turn without a dedicated wait command forces shell polling loops, risks harness command timeouts on multi-minute `merge_checks`, and leaves `CHART.md` unclosed if the turn times out or compacts before landing finishes. *Fix*: add a non-polling wait command (watching open/closed state across `keepPlace` retries and owner move) plus a `direct-route.md` re-entry check for an already-merged leaf, or wake `seat.A` when the leaf leaves `merge`.

## Item 12 `[new]` (Abandon and Growth stop to lifecycle)

- **Door deleting `issues/open/<chart-slug>` leaks loaded `leafSlice` and bypasses `.lock`**: `src/reconcile.ts` `reconcileHost` intentionally ignores any loaded `akrogon-l*.slice` matching no leaf (`#151`). Deleting `issues/open/<chart-slug>` directly after `run-check` loaded `leafSlice` strands that slice permanently and bypasses `flockHold(owner/.lock)` / `enclosingCapture` (`src/pack.ts`). *Fix*: run abandon through the akrogon verb (cancelling runs, stopping `leafSlice`, removing unlocked worktree and branch, and deleting `issues/open/<owner>` under the global lock).
- **Growth stop to lifecycle reusing the same `<slug>` corrupts `log.jsonl` and `timings.jsonl`**: Deleting `issues/open/<chart-slug>/<slug>` and handing off a new lifecycle leaf with the exact same `<slug>` merges the aborted direct attempt's prior `log.jsonl` and `timings.jsonl` records into the new leaf, corrupting `src/status.ts` `starts` / `leafJson` `history`, `src/phase.ts` `commitMove`'s `merge_stamp` count, and `src/timing-report.ts` `visitsOf`. *Fix*: either give the lifecycle handoff a fresh slug (renaming the branch/worktree) or convert the existing leaf in place (clearing `direct` and `seat`, moving to `plan.synthesis`) instead of deleting and recreating under the same slug.

## Item 15 `[new]` (`commandMerged` broadcast gate)

- **Empty `webhook_env: []` still passes a presence check**: `src/config.ts` `repoSchema` defines `broadcast: z.object({ discord: z.object({ webhook_env: z.array(text) }) }).optional()` without `.min(1)`, whereas `skills/broadcast-issue/SKILL.md` `## Send` treats an empty list as an error. *Fix*: gate `src/phase.ts` `commandMerged` on `(repo.config.broadcast?.discord.webhook_env.length ?? 0) > 0` (or require `.min(1)` in `repoSchema`), and note that existing `land`/`broadcastPass` test fixtures whose repos omit `broadcast` will need `broadcast` configured.

## Item 17 `[new]` (Waiting before restarting consultants)

- **Gaps on reused (operator-supplied) consultants**: Item 17 gates only *restarting* consultants, not *reusing* an operator-supplied B on the next chart. Because `hc send --if` accepts only `idle` or `working` (not `done`), `broadcastPass` sends after a `terminal()` check; if the door moves to the next chart without waiting for `broadcast pending` to clear and B's broadcast turn to end, the door's next `hc send` (sent from cached wake state under `questions.md` `## Blind peer exchange` `Asking`) can collide with the in-flight broadcast and return `started: false`, triggering a hard error. *Fix*: wait until `broadcast pending` clears and B's broadcast turn ends before **reusing or restarting** B.
