# Slot C rebuttal: release refresh

Disagreements only. Checked against main at 74472d3 (`git fetch` run; HEAD equals origin/main).

- Q1 is not delivered for parked as a rule, only as a side effect. The merged pick ("no new work; record Q1 as delivered by #93") rests on park refusing leaves with a tab or worktree (src/park.ts `running`), which proves a parked leaf has no live seats but says nothing about deleting its temp: parked leaves are absent from the leaf map reconcile builds its scratch rules from (`allLeaves` spans open and closed only, src/state.ts:153-155), so a parked leaf's temp dir falls into the unowned branch and is deleted only when no leaf of the whole repo is running (src/reconcile.ts:987 through `dropUnowned`, :744-750), and the per-repo slice step never sees a parked leaf's slice at all (src/reconcile.ts:426-439 iterates `leaves`). On a busy repo a parked leaf's temp therefore sits indefinitely, which is the opposite of release on park. Fix: include parked leaves in the scratch map with finished-leaf semantics for temp and slice only (tab, slice-process and `dropChecked` vetoes unchanged), or record in the fork's Taken that parked temp is deliberately treated as unowned junk. I accept this is a small, rare remainder because the park gate means the usual parked leaf was never dispatched.
- The delivered claim for temp deletion overstates the slice check in one case the merged notes do not name: when the unit inventory cannot be read, a finished leaf's temp is deleted without its slice being stopped or proven empty. `loadedUnits` returns null when the manager cannot be asked and its own comment says callers treat that as "stop nothing" (src/reconcile.ts:114, `loadedUnits`), the slice step then skips the stop (src/reconcile.ts:431), but the temp branch only consults the slice when `loadedSlices !== null` and otherwise falls through to `dropChecked` (src/reconcile.ts:966-973). `dropChecked` vetoes processes using the temp folder, so this cannot delete under a process inside temp, but Q1's order (stop processes, confirm exit, then delete) is not met for slice processes working elsewhere. Fix: treat `loadedSlices === null` like `live === null` for finished leaves' temp (keep with an inventory-unavailable reason), matching how both are already treated as unproven everywhere else in the pass.
