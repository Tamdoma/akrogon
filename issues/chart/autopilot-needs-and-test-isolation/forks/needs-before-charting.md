## Taken

A's pick under full autopilot, 2026-11-09 (signposts 5a, operator direction 2026-11-05; values: simplicity, removing over adding):

Q1. One needs sheet per door session, covering every handed chart, completed with the signposts round before the first map, in every mode, its rows answered only by the operator. The first reply previews it and no longer excludes approvals. Rows: every foreseeable key, login, file, account, resource and permission, human-only action, and operator-only authorization: grants for live changes and paid or nested launches, paid budget, fixture retention, secret-save, and closes that need an operator decision (C rebuttal 1; delivered-identity closes the door runs under Open are not rows). A grant row names operation, identity, targets and bounds; a field the operator was not asked to approve is not approved, and a leaf's `grants` record binds only inside what was approved (B R1). A route whose authority cannot be named by then is unavailable under the sheet. "none" when empty. Charting proceeds while rows are pending; only the affected proofs and handoff wait; a pending row is never re-asked as a question. A chart added to the session after the signposts round (a changed-list yes) gets its rows asked with that proposal, before its charting (C rebuttal 2). Canonical record in every mode: a `## Needs` list in the session's forks/signposts.md original, one line per row with status (pending, supplied, late), re-copied verbatim to the session's charts like any correction; autopilot `wait:` lines reference rows (B R2, C rebuttal 3).

Q2. A pending row is an ordinary wait. A new need or wider authority found later is a late find: if a useful outcome stays inside recorded authority, A continues it under Take's cut rule; otherwise the chart parks, drain moves on, and at that moment the door writes one terminal statement and runs one `akrogon notice --attention` naming the chart, what blocks it and the record path (never a secret value), records the notice path or its failed result on the row, and adds the row as `late`. The notice reports a blocker; it is not a new approval request (B). Once per park, never per pass or on resume. Outside hc or in an unregistered repo: terminal statement and record only (B R2). The operator's own next message resumes the chart (existing).
