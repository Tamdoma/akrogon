- The first reply's best-guess list stops excluding approvals; it becomes the draft of one sheet. Take's later needs sheet becomes the mapping and presence check of what the upfront sheet asked (leaf `inputs[].steps`, `grants` bound inside the approved scope); naming a leaf creates no new authority (B).

## Q2 pick: a late find continues a route inside recorded authority, else parks once with an immediate notice; never a silent cut
