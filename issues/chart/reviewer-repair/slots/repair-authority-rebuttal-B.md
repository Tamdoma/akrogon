2. **X2. I do not endorse the attributed claim that self-preference is “weak or absent when a real check decides.”** That conflates repair correctness under external feedback with bias in choosing or judging one's own repair (`repair-authority-merged.md:17`). A check establishes the behavior it tests. It does not establish unbiased interpretation of the criterion or unbiased selection of tests. The repair research supports useful external feedback, with variable gains and feedback-quality limits. It does not confirm absence of Sol self-preference. Evidence: [Olausson et al., ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.09896), [Chen et al., ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.881/), and [Panickssery et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13076). The general “weak or absent” claim should not carry B's attribution.

3. **X3. A failing-test commit before every fix is too broad.** The merged instruction applies to command and prose repairs as well as code (`repair-authority-merged.md:20`). Four of the five recent akrogon trips were command or documentation corrections, and no trip began with a red test (`repair-authority-merged.md:4`). Require appropriate before/after evidence, and a regression test when it meaningfully verifies the defect. Do not require a prose-pinning test or a separate failing-test commit for every repair. Evidence: `skills/implement-issue/SKILL.md:53` permits proportionate verification, including no new test for trivial changes, and `skills/check-issue/SKILL.md:47` rejects missing-test findings based on brittle prose assertions.

4. **X4. Phase residence does not locate testing time.** The implementation/merge percentages do not establish that the operator's testing time sits in those phases (`repair-authority-merged.md:6`). Transition timestamps include coding, waiting and verification without separating them. Evidence: `issues/log.jsonl:400-407` records phase transitions, not individual check durations. Keep the phase-time shares, but remove the claim that they measure where testing time is spent.
