- **Q7. When should the watch consult?** A known base test failure can repeat on every recovery. Consulting on first evidence of a shared defect or only after routine recovery is exhausted changes both the trigger and the proof. See `skills/watch-issues/SKILL.md`, Judge.
- **Q8. Must unrelated leaves keep moving while a consultant is silent?** Waiting in the current turn holds every later timer tick. Continuing the watch requires collecting the eventual reply and checking that its leaf still needs action. See `skills/watch-issues/scripts/timer.ts`, `poll`.
- **Q9. When one base defect breaks several leaves, is that one consultation or one per leaf?** The reported Bun upgrade blocked every merge. The answer determines who follows the fix and what counts toward the Q4 limit. See `INTAKE.md`, Observation.
- **Q1 mixes who acts with where they may write.** Handing work to a chart door, charting a fix, editing main and editing lifecycle state are distinct grants. Also specify whether the consultant may execute a repair itself, since limiting only the watch leaves delegated actions undefined. See `src/seat.ts`, `startConsultant`.
- **Q2 omits who can take an out-of-scope action.** A consultant can diagnose a base defect while an existing owner performs the fix. That case needs neither new authority for the consultant nor an operator decision. See `INTAKE.md`, Observation.
- **Q3 conflicts with Q1(a) on cleanup.** Closing a consultant terminal after each answer needs an exception to the current terminal-removal rule. The existing child lifetime ends with its parent. See `skills/watch-issues/SKILL.md`, Never, and `src/seat.ts`, `startConsultant`.
