## Missing direction questions

1. **Which failure/stall classes trigger a consultant?** (a) only `phase=failed` leaves (human-prereq, unknown record, or 2-cycle cap), (b) also unsteerable/repeat-loop busy seats (e.g. notify-only codex, failed steer), (c) also repo-wide failures (`observe.ts` unreadable states, `next`/`phase` command errors, base-branch reds blocking multiple leaves).
   - Reason: Steers `Judge` routing, evidence packaging, cross-leaf grouping, and launch volume (`skills/watch-issues/SKILL.md` `## Judge`; `skills/watch-issues/scripts/observe.ts` `main`).
2. **What may the consultant itself do?** (a) read-only advisor returning diagnosis and next action, (b) advisor allowed to run read-only checks/repros in a worktree, (c) active fixer allowed to edit worktrees/base/seeds or run lifecycle verbs directly.

3. **How does the watch wait on a consultant alongside 20-minute ticks?** (a) stay in the tick turn via `hc wait --children any` (as in `skills/chart-issues/assets/questions.md` `## Blind peer exchange`), holding ticks, (b) end the turn and receive `<hc from="...">` replies out-of-band while ticks keep advancing other leaves, (c) bounded sync wait then async/next-tick check or notify.
   - Reason: Steers `skills/watch-issues/SKILL.md` `## Invocations`, `skills/watch-issues/scripts/timer.ts` `poll` (which queues dues while watcher is `working`), multi-leaf liveness, and catching silent-stop/blocked consultants.
4. **Single consultant recipe or multi-consultant / escalation, and what config scope?** (a) one global watch-only consultant in `~/.akrogon/config.yaml`, (b) one global recipe shared with `akrogon seat --slot` / `chart-issues` and optionally overridable per repo in `issues/config.yaml`, (c) ordered escalation chain or parallel panel.
   - Reason: Steers `src/config.ts` (`globalSchema`, `repoSchema`, `seats`), `src/akrogon.ts` (`seat` case), and whether `chart-issues` is touched at all.
5. **How are consultant decisions audited and caps tracked across ticks/compactions?** (a) conversation memory only (caps reset on compaction), (b) durable append-only record (e.g. `issues/log.jsonl` or hc labels) so the operator can audit unattended actions and caps survive compaction.
   - Reason: Steers `skills/watch-issues/SKILL.md` `## Never` (no editing `issues/`), cap reliability, and operator review after returning.

## Issues in draft questions

- **Q1 (mis-optioned / conflicts with intake & Q3a):** Option (a) keeps `skills/watch-issues/SKILL.md` `## Never` intact (forbidding killing hc terminals other than the timer, which blocks Q3a) and cannot resolve either motivating case in `INTAKE.md` (base red on `main`, blocked leaf needing a fix leaf); it also skips intermediate actions (`park`, `seed-issue`, messaging an open chart door, overriding human-prereq/2-cycle stops) and conflates watch authority with consultant authority.
- **Q2 (mis-optioned / coupled to Q1):** Option (a) ("everything else is the consultant's call") contradicts Q1(a) whenever a fix exceeds existing watch verbs, and the options skip a middle boundary for destructive/host/contract changes (pushing to `main` outside merge, changing host tooling, or altering a leaf brief).
- **Q3 (mis-optioned):** Assumes one single-turn consultant, omitting multi-turn back-and-forth (lock: "Questions and answers go both ways over `hc send`"), concurrent failures in one tick, and cleanup on `watch-issues stop` (`skills/watch-issues/scripts/stop.ts` `main` kills only the timer, while `src/seat.ts` `startConsultant` binds `outlive: false` to the watcher).
- **Q4 (strawman option):** Option (b) ("no limit") is a strawman for an unattended 20-minute loop spawning paid agents; the real trade-off is which dimensions are capped (turns per consult, consults per failure, per watch, wait timeout) and whether hitting a cap notifies or escalates.
- **Q5 (against the lock / impossible option):** Option (b) ("skill only") cannot satisfy the lock (`~/.akrogon/config.yaml` following `slots:` + `harnesses:`) because `src/config.ts` `globalSchema` and `globalSchema.slots` are `z.strictObject`, so any added key breaks `readGlobal()` everywhere; `src/akrogon.ts` (`seat` case) also requires `--harness`/`--model`/`--effort` and `src/hc.ts` `consultantName` (`<prefix>-slot-<letter>`) collides with an open chart door's consultants.
- **Q6 (inaccurate rationale in option b):** The live-vs-stand-in proof choice is valid for the paid grant, but `watch-any-harness` proved timer ticks (`skills/watch-issues/scripts/timer.ts`), not consultant launch/start-proof (`src/seat.ts` `startConsultant`) or `hc send` / `hc wait --children` round-trips alongside a running timer.
