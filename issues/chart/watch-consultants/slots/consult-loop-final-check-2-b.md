- **F1. Items 4′ and 6 can strand the default agy helper at startup.** Taskless `startConsultant` returns the address before `firstTask` or `agyTask` runs. On a startup permission screen, ordinary `hc send` cannot deliver the ask, and a replacement can hit the same screen. The existing agy startup path handles that screen and waits for readiness before it sends.
- **F2. Items 4′ and 8′ conflict on operator messages.** Item 4′ says to ignore wakes from every address except the consultant; item 8′ accepts the operator’s words as a reason to reopen. An operator instruction arriving during a wait must reach the watcher, including an explicit stop.
- **F3. Items 1 and 8′ conflict after manual recovery.** A successful `akrogon phase` out of failed clears the failure record (`src/phase.ts`, `commitMove`). Item 8′ says this reopens the case, although item 1 now excludes the leaf. Reconcile it as already resumed.
- **F4. Items 6 and 6′ allow a replacement to repeat a successful write.** A helper can push a fix and exit before sending its report. Item 7′ reconciles on watch stop and item 11 on watch restart, but neither covers helper replacement in the same watch. Inspect the worktree, remote and leaf state before reauthorizing the repair.
