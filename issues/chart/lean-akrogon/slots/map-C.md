## M2. Backpressure & queue dynamics
- **Do merge leaves count against `max_active`?** **No** (`src/next.ts:380-381`, changed in `633db0ad` / C4) `[high]`.
- **Does `refill` (`8ef5c061`/`436b5179`) start new leaves while the merge queue is deep?** **Yes** (`src/next.ts:831-871` → `dispatchLeaf` → `capRefusal` `:410-418` ignores `merge`). Every leaf entering a backed-up merge queue immediately frees a slot and spawns another active leaf — piling up 17+ worktrees (34 GB), running leaf verifies/seats beside the stalled merge lane, and staling queued leaves into conflicts (`891 h` queue wait vs `243 h` land runs) `[high]`.
- **Queue-depth admission vs P10/P14:** A **per-repo merge-queue cap** (e.g., block *new* leaf allocation in repo R when R has `>= 2` leaves waiting in `merge`, while letting already-seated leaves finish and other repos run) is ~10 stateless lines in `src/next.ts:410-418` vs hundreds/thousands of lines in P10/P14. Unlike reverting `633db0ad` (which starved all repos when one repo's lane stalled, C4), a per-repo merge-queue cap keeps 1–2 leaves buffered for zero lane starvation, caps WIP & disk/RAM footprint on any PC, and cuts rebase churn `[high]`.
