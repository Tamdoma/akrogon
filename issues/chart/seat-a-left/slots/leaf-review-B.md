# Leaf draft review — Slot B

1. Criterion 4 conflicts with the replacement-only contract. It promises “A left of B, no swap and no focus call” for every tab with both seats present or an extra operator pane. Present seats can be manually reversed and must remain unchanged under the binding exclusion of normalization. After an unapplied swap failure, criterion 3 deliberately leaves both seats recorded with A on the right, and the next pass must reuse them without retrying. Also, the existing extra-operator-pane cases include missing A (`tests/next.test.ts:1856-1889`), which must perform the new swap and focus restoration under criterion 1. Current allocation uses recorded IDs regardless of member order (`src/next.ts:414-428`). Exact fix: separate the cases. Require fresh/bootstrap and B-only allocation to keep their existing A-before-B result without swap/focus calls. Require both-present seats to preserve existing IDs and positions, including manually reversed or failed-swap positions, without swap/focus calls. Require extra operator panes to retain their IDs and positions while missing-A/surviving-B replacement still runs the repair, subject to the accepted focus cost.
