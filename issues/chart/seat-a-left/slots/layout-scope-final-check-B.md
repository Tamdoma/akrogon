# Final-shape check — Slot B

1. Lost acknowledgement does not establish that A remains on the right. Probe step 6 shows an applied source=B swap puts A left. If that successful operation loses its reply, A is recorded on the left despite the command error. Exact fix: record “on swap error, preserve the recorded A ID, report the error and do not retry or close; its position may be right if unapplied or left if the reply was lost.” Saving identity prevents duplication, but must not assert an unobserved position.

2. Source=B proves focus preservation only when B was already the focused pane. Probe step 6 does not cover an extra operator pane focused within the same leaf tab. Existing allocation explicitly supports extra panes (`tests/next.test.ts:1856-1889`). Source=B selects B, and the proposed restoration skips the same-tab case, so that operator can lose pane focus even without switching tabs during the stated window. Exact fix: probe the same-tab extra-pane case before claiming preservation, then either preserve its original focused pane through a proven operation or carry this additional focus change as an explicit operator-approved cost. The accepted tab-switch window alone does not cover it.
