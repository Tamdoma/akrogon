5. Restore: reading the focused tab before swap (`herdr tab list`, `.focused`) and running `herdr tab focus <prev>` after swap restored tab and workspace focus (w8:t7X, and w8:t6Z after the wG run).
Cleanup: `herdr tab close <probe tab>` after each run; `herdr tab list` shows no probe tab (index null) and the probe pane IDs absent. Cleanup result: all six probe tabs removed.
