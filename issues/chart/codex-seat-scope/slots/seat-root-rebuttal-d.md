# Rebuttal, slot D: fork seat-root

R1. Merged O3 cost line, "created by startSeat after firstTask confirms", misstates map-D. D2 arms on `confirmed` and on `unconfirmed`, never on `failed`. Read literally, a live-but-unconfirmed seat would never arm and tool kill would be dead for exactly the seats whose start proof is already degraded. Evidence: map-d.md section D2; `startSeat` in `src/seat.ts` continues driving the seat after an `unconfirmed` outcome.

R2. Merged pitfall "Fork-and-wait wrappers: O1/O2 strand the agent in the supervisor's `t-`, and scopeEntry refuses loudly" is not guaranteed for O2. `scopeEntry` in `src/seat.ts` reads only `cgroupOf(launched.pid)`. In the fork shape where the pid-preserving supervisor stays at the scope root and a forked bash launcher (pid different from `AKROGON_SEAT_PID`) mints its own `t-` and execs the agent there, `scopeEntry` sees the supervisor at the root and enters: no refusal, and the agent is then killable by `sweepScope` in `src/tool-cgroup.ts` at the next phase move whenever its `akrogon/<t>` is unpopulated. Consequence: if A picks O2, its fork-wrapper residual must be recorded as silent in that shape, not loud. The observed launchers today are exec chains, which O2 covers and C proved; this residual is about the next wrapper, which is what O3's ancestry timing covers for free.

R3. Against C 2A (O2 trampoline for every kind) if the lean adopts it. Only kinds akrogon wires with the sourced `BASH_ENV` can self-place at launch. In `startSeat`'s wiring in `src/seat.ts`, claude gets the executed `CLAUDE_CODE_SHELL` wrapper, which is invoked for its children and never for its own launcher, and agy already execs through its own `sh` wrap, where folding in the export costs nothing. Putting claude through a new trampoline changes its launch argv for zero mechanism and widens the test surface (`tests/tool-entry.test.ts` per-kind argv assertions). Scope the trampoline to the `BASH_ENV` kinds, plus the agy fold-in on the wrap that already exists.
