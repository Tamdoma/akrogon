F1. failed-refusal Q2 assumes every failed leaf has failure.reason and failure.phase. src/state.ts stateSchema permits phase=failed with failure absent, and watch-issues already treats that as failed=unknown. The prescribed error/recovery text cannot be constructed for that valid state. Refuse it too, explicitly say the failure record is unavailable, and omit a recovery phase that cannot be known. Do not dereference failure or print an invented/undefined phase. This is separate from the standing disagreement about durable records.

F2. blocked-prompts Q2 needs to end the blocked episode on seat replacement and phase move, including when no intermediate non-blocked status is observed. A per-slot pending/shown field has no address identity. In src/next.ts dispatchSlot, missing/finished-seat replacement happens before observeBusy. src/seat.ts startSeat does not pass its observed working status through observeBusy. If old A is blocked, dies, and replacement A is blocked by the next pass, the old marker suppresses the new seat's attention notice. src/phase.ts commitMove spreads state and clearBusyNotices only clears existing busy_notified, so the new marker can also survive phase changes unless explicitly added to cleanup. Specify removal of the old pending blocked notice and clearing blocked_notified on replacement and phase moves, as well as on a live seat leaving blocked. Prove replacement-blocked and phase-change-blocked cases. This preserves the picked field shape without adding another identity field.
