## Lifetime pitfalls and their removal

- R1: Green evidence for the wrong commit or mutated setup. Bind result to candidate SHA, base SHA, effective commands/setup and declared inputs, retain current tested-top/fast-forward guards. Fresh checkout alone does not enforce agent execution.
- R2: Health state outlives its SHA or manual pause. Separate merge health from operator pause, require explicit comparable recovery evidence, and reconcile on dispatch/restart. Do not use elapsed time as a health verdict.
- R3: Batch blame becomes leaf punishment. Record tested membership and attribution before counting repair rounds. A red stack proves a stack failed, not that every member is defective.
- R4: Prioritization revokes a running holder. Freeze holder/attempt ownership until completion, split or deliberate release. Priority chooses the next attempt only.
- R5: More upstream checking slows the merge turn through shared-host load. Move demonstrated fast checks and repair-specific proof first. Reopen host limits only with traced evidence. Preserve final gate coverage.
- R6: Metrics improve because failed work is abandoned. Compare landed leaves per occupied gate-hour alongside lead time, attributed failures, stopped work and main regressions. Do not optimize only green percentage or batch size.
- R7: Lesson pruning hides an unguarded class. Delete an active lesson only after the running guard covers every reachable case, retaining history (`skills/learn-issues/SKILL.md:20,28`). Do not derive a rigid prose checker from probabilistic artifacts.
- R8: Old long-running command processes restore obsolete behavior after a fix. Validate which revision owns active attempts and restart only at a safe reconciled boundary. This is reported in #62, not independently traced here. Attempt reconciliation must survive process replacement.

The useful first move is to protect the final gate's environment, stop proven base failures from consuming innocent leaves, and make each repair prove the rejection it addresses. More seats and more speculative batches do not remove those three causes.
