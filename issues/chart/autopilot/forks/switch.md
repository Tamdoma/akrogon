## Taken
- 2026-10-16 operator "1a | 2a | 3a" (INTAKE.md "Switch answer"):
  - Off early only by the operator saying so in words ("stop", "autopilot off"). Supplying a key or changing the work list keeps it on; a changed list is recorded as the operator's updated instruction. Once off, A makes no further choice and the next question goes to the operator; an open peer turn is collected first.
  - Compaction is the same session and continues. A replacement session starts attended; the operator's words "continue autopilot" resume the same handed list. Lost consultants stay lost.
