## Material forks, in suggested order

**Q1. What does enabling the mode cover, and what ends it?** O1: current door session/pass, explicitly enabled, with fresh authorization after session replacement. O2: durable repo-wide mode inherited by future sessions. O3: named chart only. **Pick O1**, carrying the operator's invocation and scope in current chart records so compaction retains it, but replacement sessions return attended. O2 risks forgotten authority affecting unrelated future work. O3 repeatedly interrupts multi-chart passes. O1 costs re-enabling after replacement. Define off as revoking subsequent delegated decisions and handoff authorization once received, not rolling back decisions or killing already-running lifecycle work. If the operator requires externally enforceable immediate cancellation, that is a larger mechanism to decide explicitly, not a promise a skill can make.

**Q2. Which intake may it decide?** O1: intake/destinations named at enable time, including necessary dependencies, with unrelated discoveries left off route. O2: drain every open chart and arriving seed. **Pick O1**: it makes the delegated outcome finite without inventing a work generator. O2 invites scope and spend growth as agents discover more work. **Q3. How does it choose the best solution and settle disagreement?** O1: take the recommendation automatically. O2 costs deliberation and leaves A accountable. A concrete missing fact, contradicted lock or unproved operation remains unresolved work, not a vote.
