# Loopholes rebuttal, slot C

1. **A's pick on item 10 does not follow from Picking 1a, and its "matches today's rule" claim is wrong.** Picking 1a names `failure`, `blocked` and refused prompts. It does not name `budget`. Today's rule has no one-rerun cap: an unproven budget is reported before any rerun, every other budget is rerun at once, and "a kind with no proven signal never fails on idle" (`questions.md:46`). Dropping a peer after one extra wait treats an explicitly unproven signal as a failure. That may be the right policy, but it is a new rule, not a derivation, so "no new operator question" does not hold. It also needs one more line: a return file arriving after the drop is recorded as ignored, or a later door can read it as a completed exchange.

2. **Corrections 3 and 4 use "held" for two different states.** Correction 3 ends the mode when every item is "handed off, closed or held". Correction 4 keeps the mode on for a chart waiting on a need. In this skill `Held` is also a terminal marker meaning abandon (`SKILL.md:85`). An implementer reading correction 3 alone can end the mode on a merely waiting chart. Fix: correction 3 names the terminal markers (`Handed off`, `Closed`, `Held`) and says a waiting chart is none of them.

3. **Correction 15 defines "limits" too narrowly, and it cuts against N1.** As merged, limits means the handed list, consultants and wait rules. But the direct protocol's own bounds, `fix_rounds` and the two-push-attempt cap (`SKILL.md:112,119`), are limits in the ordinary sense, and N1's argument from B is precisely that repeated "one more round" collides with "never changes its own limits". Both cannot stand as written. Fix: either correction 15 includes existing numeric bounds in the skill, or N1 drops the limits argument and rests only on abandon and the charting-only scope words.

4. **N1's framing is wider than the unresolved intent.** Direct availability with B was inside the drafts the operator approved at handoff (`autopilot-mode/brief.md:4` route as an A answer; `design.md:13` K2 only removes it for A alone), before he asked for loopholes. What those drafts never settled is the continuations: repeated rounds past the bound and abandon. Presenting "lifecycle only" as the question reopens an approved leaf outcome. Fix: N1 asks only whether direct's post-bound choices are A's, with lifecycle-only as one answer, not as the framing of the whole item.
