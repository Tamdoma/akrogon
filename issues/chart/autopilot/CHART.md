# Chart: autopilot

## Destination
A non-default autopilot mode for the chart-issues door: when the operator switches it on in one door tab, that door runs every step of today's charting process and answers the operator's decision points itself, then hands off; the operator-run watcher takes over after handoff. Attended doors in other tabs are unaffected. Repo: akrogon.

Route: lifecycle (debate no)

Opening map: slots/map-merged.md (A, B, C blind maps and history research).

## Forks taken
- [Boundary](forks/boundary.md): A answers every question with the consultants named; handed work only; outside-account approvals and its own rules stay with the operator; best-guess needs list at start.
- [Picking](forks/picking.md): A decides standing disagreements and records dissent; any consultant count; a failed consultant is dropped for the session and named in the morning record.
- [Switch](forks/switch.md): on in words at start; off only in words; a restarted session starts attended until "continue autopilot"; handed list only; consultants not shared.
- [Watcher seam](forks/watcher-seam.md): door starts nothing; the watcher picks up handed-off leaves; no review wait; a handed failed leaf is charted like other work.
- [Morning record](forks/morning-record.md): Autopilot section per chart written as A goes; breaches first; first run with the operator around; no score.

- [Loopholes](forks/loopholes.md): no direct route in autopilot; a silent consultant gets one more wait, then is dropped; leftover main steps wait for the operator to reopen a door.
