# Slot B final-shape check

Evidence below is inspected source and control-flow inference, checked 2026-10-27. No implementation was changed or runtime test run.

**C1. Point 5 contradicts reconcile's live-run veto.** `src/reconcile.ts:sliceKeepReason` returns `live run record` before checking whether the leaf's tab survives. `stopSlice` repeats that veto under the admission lock. `src/admission.ts:liveRuns` includes both live and waiting checks. Therefore placing the service in the leaf slice does not make tab disappearance stop it during a check or admission wait. With current per-check records, it may instead be stopped between checks, making lifetime depend on timing. Either retain survival after tab loss or explicitly change the reconciliation rule for detached verification and prove tab-loss behavior during a check, during admission wait and between checks. Slice placement alone does not implement the chosen policy.

**C2. Point 3 needs a terminal path without end.** “Waits for that pass's end” cannot be the only completion condition while point 6 preserves incomplete receipts after termination. A pass cancelled through point 2, killed by reconcile under point 5, or failed before normal completion may never write `end`. The waiter must also observe service termination, return failure for an incomplete receipt and stop joining that dead pass. With `CollectMode=inactive-or-failed`, account for the service having already been collected before a repeat caller arrives. Sources: `src/verify.ts:runOnce` assigns `end` only after normal completion and saves it conditionally in finally; `src/land.ts:launchLand`/`landAlive` demonstrate collected services and separate liveness observation. This needs no receipt reason field or signal handler.

**C3. Points 1–2 leave direct-holder ownership and cancellation undefined.** Not every supported verify caller has a leaf. `src/verify.ts:verifyCaller` delegates ordinary calls to `src/run-check.ts:leafTarget`, whose direct path returns `slug: checkout` with the supplied holder target. `checkedRun` places these runs in `akrogon.slice`, rather than a leaf slice. Today's `src/stop-run.ts:stopRunCommand` authorizes by caller scope and accepts no holder argument. Specify the service placement and cancellation authority for `verify --holder`, preserving distinct holder targets rather than treating every checkout owner as the same leaf. Leaf ownership is also a deliberate change from today's pane-scope authorization, not an existing stop-run guarantee. Keep ordinary run-check cancellation scoped as today unless separately changing that policy. The four checkout cancellations established in the opening map make holder coverage part of this fix.

Points 4, 6 and 7: none. Other parts of points 1–3: none.
