For either option, preserve the five observation sections. Any supported suspicion names its author (reporter or agent), the mechanism and supporting evidence, each file read with its location and installed/vendor status, and known contrary evidence or an unresolved distinguishing check. Several conditions may be listed without claiming they explain every symptom. Missing information stays “Not provided,” and an unresolved outcome names the evidence needed next without running that check.

- **1a (recommended)** Always include `Suspected root cause`. Record the supported suspicion, or say that no supported hypothesis was found and give the inspection outcome and actionable gap. This makes the required attempt visible in standalone reports. Cost: a short extra section even when the cause remains unknown.
- **1b** Include `Suspected root cause` only when a hypothesis is supported. For an unresolved case, append the inspection outcome, files read and actionable gap to Location, explicitly labeled as agent investigation rather than observation. This avoids an empty cause heading, but spreads investigation evidence across the existing report sections.

Pitfalls avoided: Both options attribute claims, preserve symptoms and identify consumer copies, so a path in Stopsol cannot silently become evidence that the framework's current source has the same defect. Done-criteria cover no supported hypothesis, a reporter suspicion contradicted by inspected evidence, and unrelated symptoms, with charting independently reassessing every causal claim. Neither option requires inventing contrary evidence or a hypothesis to complete the report.
