- **F1.** The draft assumes the timer publishes its label before `tick.ts` reads it. Input reaches the watcher before `send` returns, and hc can wait seconds to determine `started`. A fast gate can therefore reject a legitimate tick or consume an old label. The proof needs both orderings, including a gate that starts before publication. Evidence: `tests/fixtures/hc-schema.json` (`SendResult`), `timer.ts` and `tick.ts`.
- **F2.** A label keyed only by due cannot distinguish a late first copy from its retry. Both carry the same timer, PID and due. If the retry is marked accepted before the late first copy reads the label, that copy can stamp the receipt and suppress the retry. Bind the outcome to the individual send attempt. Evidence: `timer.ts` tick text and `tick.ts` stale-due check.
- **F3.** The reply reports the program’s last status word when text went in. It supplies no input-owner identity. A person’s input can reach the harness before it reports `working`, and a null status means no reported state. P5 observed an already-working harness; it does not prove that `idle` or `done` identifies a tick-owned turn in the race S4a addresses. Evidence: `SendResult.status` and `timer-design.md` P5.
- **F4.** “Resends that due once as its own turn” promises a successful retry. The retry can race another person’s turn too. Criterion 4 requires one further attempt followed by a drop if still unreceipted; accepting both attempts into person turns must run zero checks. Evidence: leaf `brief.md` criterion 4 and `timer.ts` two-attempt limit.
- **F5.** The existing stand-in finishes its hook before reading the next line. Merely setting it to `working` before delivery can produce a passing “no check in that turn” even with the gate removed. Exercise the gate while the simulated person turn is still active and show that bypassing the gate makes the test fail. Evidence: `tests/fixtures/watch-agent.sh` and `tests/stand-in.sh` (`turn` and input loop).
- **F6.** S4a accepts interference in a person’s watcher turn. It does not extend criterion 7’s shell-exit exception beyond a held draft. A harness can still exit between the pin read and an unguarded send without a draft. The remaining shell can receive the text before any label or tick gate can act. Evidence: `timer.ts` final `readWatcher` and `send`, leaf `brief.md` criterion 7, operator S2a/S3a.
