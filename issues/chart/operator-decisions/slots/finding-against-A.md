# Fork notes, slot A: finding-against, 2026-11-19

## Q1 What counts, manual
- Pick: a finding is a fact with evidence that the decision as written cannot work or causes harm: a cited file, line or command is gone or different, its done-test cannot pass, it clashes with another locked decision, or a proof under `## Take` fails. Bar: check-issue's Fix bar (a concrete consequence today, never a preference). The door researches no alternative design. Recorded under the decision fork's `## Findings`. Manual: the next round shows it as one keep-or-change question with the evidence; keep = chart on as recorded; change = the operator's words as a dated correction. Charting around the decision continues meanwhile. Reason: the operator's 2a correction ("if we find out … not good … that's still fine. But the initial idea has to be the one that we are charting around").
- Rejected: ordinary fork with a door recommendation (re-asks by finding); show only at handoff review (late, wasted charting).
- Pitfall: re-litigation by findings; removed by the Fix bar and no-alternative rule.

## Q2 Autopilot
- Pick: A never changes the decision. The finding is recorded, carried into each affected leaf design as a held disagreement, named under CHART.md `## Autopilot` and in the pass-end line. When the decision cannot hand off as stated (a failed proof cuts the outcome), the existing cut rule parks the chart for the operator. Otherwise no wait.
- Rejected: any finding parks the chart (recreates the stall).

## Q3 Conflicts and ambiguity
- Pick: operator-only in every mode. Two counting decisions that conflict, or a seed decision against a round answer: shown in one line and asked (manual); under autopilot the chart waits as an operator question (existing park and notice). Ambiguous decision words: manual, asked; autopilot, A records its reading labelled as A's when both readings give the same leaf, and the chart waits when they give different leaves. Reason: an operator decision is never replaced by A's pick. questions.md's "an unresolved conflict about a fork becomes a fork answered per mode" gets the exception for conflicts between operator decisions.
- Rejected: A picks the newer one (dates do not show intent).
