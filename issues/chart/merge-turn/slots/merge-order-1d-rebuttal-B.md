- **F1. Step 3 changes publication order and is not agreed by B.** Putting the earliest holder last means later carried leaves appear before it in Git history and move to merged first. Step 4's claim that “FIFO publication order holds” is therefore false for a successful batch (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:8-9,12`). This may be an acceptable batch policy, but it needs an explicit choice to preserve FIFO admission while allowing different commit/completion order. It also means the holder's own leaf must be integrated after all carried changes, rather than being the first checked integration. Do not attribute that change to prior consensus.

- **F2. Step 4 handles holder conflict but loses the carried-member solo rule.** Restoring the entire unpublished stack and letting the holder resolve solo now is coherent. However, a carried member that conflicts is merely dropped (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:9`), with no solo mark. The next snapshot can repeatedly carry and drop it instead of giving it the previously promised conflict-resolution turn. Persist solo eligibility for a dropped conflict member, and exclude solo-marked leaves from future batches even when a different, unmarked holder starts one. The batch snapshot rule at `:7` and red-only marking at `:13` do not specify that exclusion.

- **F3. Step 6 removes a needed per-member proof on an incorrect invariant.** “A clean rebase does not change its files” (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:11`) is false: integration incorporates earlier changes and can automatically change the resulting file contents without a textual conflict. Also, the current move into merge did not check an independently stored member range. `src/phase.ts:282-295` checks the diff and trailers against the remote, and an earlier member's trailer can satisfy a later member's change to the same test path in a cumulative stack. Preserve each member's original base/head and validate its final own range against the stored predecessor, with a final cumulative top check as well. A prior per-leaf guard is not sufficient proof of the new integrated range.

- **F4. Step 5 permits independent member work instead of coordinator authority.** Allowing a leaf to do merge work because it is a recorded member (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:10`) weakens exclusion. Carried members' B seats must remain unprompted and unable to launch independent integration or publication. The holder must be authorized to operate on their recorded ranges, rather than every member becoming an owner. Today `src/phase.ts:211-215` validates a slot but not the invoking pane, and `src/next.ts:627-628` dispatches required seats per leaf. Both entry paths need the same batch-owner rule.

- **F5. Q2 2a remains my recommendation, but does not prove step 10's bound.** `max_active` counts live allocation (`src/next.ts:305-315`) and prevents new tabs only at allocation (`:334-336`). A merge record can outlive its tab, and phase transitions allow worktree-less states (`src/phase.ts:225-229`). Lowering max_active also does not remove existing tabs. Thus “every waiting leaf holds a tab” and “a batch never exceeds max_active” (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:15`) need an explicit membership invariant or admission cap. Keeping tabs and slots alone establishes neither. Do not turn a capacity setting into an unproven batch limit.

- **F6. Q3 3a remains my recommendation, but N+1 is not a run bound.** A failed batch followed by one solo attempt per member is N+1 initial attempts (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:18`), not total check runs. Repair, interrupted verification and outside pushes can add runs (`skills/merge-issue/SKILL.md:43,49`). Restoring and retrying solo must use each member's own saved patch boundaries, not only its pre-batch HEAD, and the durable solo exclusions in F2 must apply to every subsequent snapshot.

- **F7. Q4 4a does not remove the whole Nit-loss class as stated.** Moving the step only into check.review (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:19`) misses held or newly discovered Nits after B's check.repair pass, which can transition directly to merge (`src/routing.ts:33`). The current rule specifically records a reusable Nit B still holds at merge (`skills/merge-issue/SKILL.md:35`). Either name all final B review/repair exits that own this operation, with one recording per retained Nit, or have the holder inspect carried final reviews. I dispute recommending check.review alone as complete, not moving lesson recording out of batch merge generally.

- **F8. The batch record and cleanup protection remain incomplete.** Step 2 stores heads and the tested top (`merge-order-1d-merged.md:7`) but does not explicitly retain original patch bases, completed member transitions or protection through finalization. A carried member can complete an owner and move its folder before later transitions (`src/phase.ts:134,146-180`), and any sweep can close merged tabs and remove completed worktrees (`src/next.ts:652-658,737-740`). Step 9's explicit cleanup after landing does not disable those existing paths. Recovery needs enough durable ranges/progress to distinguish unpublished restoration from landed completion and must protect all still-needed batch resources until the coordinator finishes. Putting the holder last reduces one cleanup risk but does not eliminate these paths.
