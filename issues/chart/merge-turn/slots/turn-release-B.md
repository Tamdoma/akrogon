### 2 · If the holder stops during a batch, should the command recover the other leaves or hold the whole batch for you?

The code can mark a seat failed after undelivered prompts or an unreachable-seat error, but that state change does not itself stop a running check or push process (`src/next.ts:459-478`). - **2a (recommended)** When an observed failure or stop occurs, the command reconciles the saved batch before releasing the turn. Once the former publisher is proven stopped, it fetches the target. If the batch landed, it finishes recording the landed members without testing or pushing again. If it did not land, it restores every unpublished member to its saved base and head, keeps the holder's chosen failure or repair state, and returns unaffected carried leaves to the queue at their existing places. - **2b** Any interrupted batch waits for the operator to reconcile landing and restore or finish its members before queue release. Pitfalls avoided: Neither option treats `phase: failed` as proof that publication has stopped, restores a landed branch, reruns an already-landed batch, or automatically restarts a failed leaf. Recovery must handle termination before push, a lost push reply, and a crash between member phase moves, while keeping needed worktrees and records until cleanup is complete. For a silent busy holder, the operator stops its agent and any check/push children, then runs `akrogon next`; no elapsed-time expiry is added. Unknown child ownership or remote read failure leaves the attempt unreleased and reports exactly what remains to establish.
