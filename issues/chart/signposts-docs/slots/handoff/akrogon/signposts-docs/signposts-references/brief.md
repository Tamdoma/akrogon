# Brief: signposts-references

## What

Bring akrogon's docs and file references level with the signposts step (d6bb6429) and the three chart door modes. Text only, in `docs/reference/files.md` ("Layout of issues/"), `docs/use/plan-work.md` ("Open a chart", "Set the signposts"), `docs/use/autopilot.md`, `docs/reference/words.md` ("Chart door", "Fork"), `docs/reference/limits.md` ("What stays your job" and the product-questions bullet of "What Akrogon does not do"), `docs/start/why-it-works.md` ("A contract comes before code"), `docs/start/what-it-is.md` ("The words", Chart entry), `docs/reference/commands.md` (`akrogon status` row), `docs/reference/skills.md` (chart-issues entry), `docs/pictures/ways-to-run.svg` (autopilot label text) and `skills/AREA.md` ("Key files").

## Why

Operator, 2026-11-04: "the akrogon documentation and file references update. We haven't done it since introducing signposts." The signposts leaf updated five pages, but the file reference for chart folders still names only `CHART.md` and fork files, the user guides leave out the record's copies, corrections, the door session and the autopilot handling, `status --charts` counts the signposts record without saying so, and several pages still say the operator answers every fork, which the three modes changed.

## Done-criteria

1. A reader of `docs/reference/files.md` "Layout of issues/" learns that a chart folder holds `CHART.md`, `INTAKE.md`, `seats.yaml`, `USAGE.md`, `forks/` (with `forks/signposts.md`, the door session's signposts: the original in the chart where the round ran and a verbatim copy naming it in the session's other charts), `slots/` and, on the direct route, `direct/`, matching `skills/chart-issues/assets/shapes.md` "Chart records"; and that a handover entry in `CHART.md`'s Mode record carries the path of the session's signposts record, with the skill's "Handover" section linked for the procedure.
2. A reader of `docs/use/plan-work.md` "Set the signposts" learns what the operator sees: consultants add missed angles first; a light round of four to seven questions, each with two or three choices, a recommendation and what it steers, no research line; a short account of what the session prioritizes and gives up; that a door session lasts until a new door run (surviving waits, compaction and handovers) and a chart resumed later takes the new run's signposts; that the record's original sits in one chart with copies in the others; how a correction is recorded and when a conflict is asked; that the chart order and any drop come from the signposts; and the autopilot rule for an unanswered question. It links the skill for the rest. "Open a chart" says the first reply names the mode and who answers the signposts, and that `forks/signposts.md` is the session's record, not one of the chart's own questions.
3. A reader of `docs/use/autopilot.md` learns that switching on mid-chart with no signposts asks them first; that under autopilot the signposts round is presented and answered once, not a chart wait; that under full autopilot A may order but never drop a handed chart, and a drop it would make is recorded for the operator; that the next chart is the next one not waiting, in the signposts order; and that the `## Autopilot` record's `signposts:` line names who answered and the chart holding the original.
4. `docs/reference/words.md` "Chart door" and "Fork", `docs/reference/limits.md` "What stays your job" and the product-questions bullet, `docs/start/why-it-works.md` "A contract comes before code" and `docs/start/what-it-is.md` "The words" Chart entry no longer claim the operator answers every fork and link `docs/use/autopilot.md` for who answers per mode instead of restating the modes; only `limits.md` states locally that approvals stay the operator's in every mode.
5. `docs/reference/commands.md` `akrogon status` row says that `--charts` TAKEN counts every fork file with a non-empty Taken, the signposts record included; `docs/reference/skills.md` chart-issues entry names the signposts round and the three modes; the `docs/pictures/ways-to-run.svg` autopilot label no longer says the door answers everything, in one line of at most about 30 characters inside its existing box; `skills/AREA.md` "Key files" has a chart-issues line naming `SKILL.md` and its `assets/`.
6. `tests/docs-links.test.ts` passes (the `plan-work.md#set-the-signposts` anchor stays); `git --no-pager diff --name-only "$AKROGON_BASE"...HEAD` lists only the files named in What; `src/`, `tests/` and `issues/` are unchanged.
