# The evidence line survives every restatement

## What was not good
The operator asked for a plain-language restatement of a ten-question batch. The restatement kept the questions and options and dropped the research lines. The operator noticed the research was gone and asked why.

## What the updated way does
Every restatement, however short, keeps one evidence line per question: who found what, at which tier, and where the slots disagree. Cut the background, cut the pitfalls if asked, never the evidence. Now a rule in `question-authoring.md`.

## Why
The whole point of research before questions is that the operator decides from evidence. A restatement without it asks the operator to decide from trust.

## How it gets improved
If a restatement grows past the original, the evidence line is too long. One sentence per question is the budget.
