# Questions must be laid out for reading

## What was not good
The first destination batch rendered as one block per question: background, three options, and the reply key with no breaks and no title. The operator saw walls of text and could not find the choice.

## What the updated way does
`assets/question-authoring.md` now renders each question as a markdown heading with a short title, one to three plain sentences of background, a bulleted list with the option letters in bold and the recommended one first, and a one-line reply key. Wording follows the eli rule: written for a smart 18-year-old, one consequence per option, no jargon without a gloss.

## Why
The reader has to locate the options and the recommended one in seconds. Spacing and a title do that. The cap on background keeps the decision visible instead of the history.

## How it gets improved
If a question still reads badly, shorten the background before touching the options. If the format itself needs another rule, add one sentence to the asset, not a new asset.
