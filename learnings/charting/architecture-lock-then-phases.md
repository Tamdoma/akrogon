# Lock the architecture, then argue the phases

## What was not good

Three decisions on turn signal, state and debate count were argued one question at a time. Each answer reopened the shape of the whole, so the same design was explained four times in eli form before it held.

## What the updated way does

When a batch of decisions shares one shape, the round shows the shape first as one worked example (the five-leaf issue, the review loop) and asks the operator to accept or challenge the shape. Once accepted, each phase is discussed as a variation inside it, and no later question may reopen the shape.

## Why

The operator said "I really like the architecture, let's stick to it" only after seeing the whole flow on one example. Questions asked before that were answered against a shape the operator could not yet see.

## How it improves the process

One shape round, then phase rounds. The eli restatements that re-explained the design become one worked example in the batch itself.
