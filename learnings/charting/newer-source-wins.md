# Newer research wins within a tier

## What was not good

Research returns named a tier but not a date. A 2021 book and a 2026 benchmark counted the same, and on agent workflows a six-month-old source can already be wrong.

## What the updated way does

Every finding carries its date. Within a tier the newer source wins when two disagree, twelve months for general topics, six months for agent workflows, harness behavior and model capability. No score, no weights, one rule.

## Why

Operator, 2026-09-08: "Always prioritize newer research, especially for agentic questions... a little bit more scoring to the more recent things... because everything is moving so fast."

## How it improves the process

A recommendation built on stale practice is caught by the date on its evidence line instead of by the operator.
