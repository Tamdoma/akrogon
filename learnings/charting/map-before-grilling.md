# The territory map finds forks the intake missed

## What was not good
The intake was 292 lines of first-person notes. Grilling straight from it would only have re-asked what the notes already contained.

## What the updated way does
The first chart turn presents the map in three parts, main forks, practitioner questions, beginner mistakes, and only then the first batch. The map surfaced forks the intake never mentions: what the driver does with a pane herdr reports as `blocked` on a permission prompt, whether a re-prompted skill can resume safely, and what survives a reboot of herdr.

## Why
The map is written from first principles about the domain, not from the notes. Its job is to find what the operator did not think to write down.

## How it gets improved
Keep the map proportional. Fourteen forks and ten practitioner questions was right for a rebuild of the whole lifecycle. A one-feature chart gets a map of five lines.
