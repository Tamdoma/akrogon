# Lock text against a derived criterion

Leaf chart-issues, 2026-09-11.

## Case

Brief criterion 5 forbade any reference to "parking" in the chart-issues assets. The same leaf's design required the standing creation-locked lines to be copied verbatim, and one of those lines says an operator-owned chunk "parks at dispatch before any seat spawns". Both could not hold. Plan D7 chose the verbatim lock, kept an interpretation paragraph beside the block, and both reviews recorded the residual quotation as a known limitation instead of certifying criterion 5.

## Evidence

`skills/chart-issues/assets/standing-design.md:10`, `issues/open/akrogon-loop/doors/chart-issues/{brief.md,design.md,plan.md,review-A.md,review-B.md}`.

## Learning

A done-criterion written as a forbidden-word grep can collide with text the same handoff copies verbatim. The door's pre-write audit should test each grep-shaped criterion against the verbatim blocks the design carries before the leaf opens.
