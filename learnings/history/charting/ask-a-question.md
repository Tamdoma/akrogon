# Ask a real question, not a topic

## What was not good
Chart skill version 1 shaped every question as a topic label and a statement about a decision. The operator read "First harness pair and who operates" and "Prove the handoff before opening leaves" and could not tell what was being asked. The skill said "define jargon" but never said the heading has to be a question.

## What the updated way does
Version 2 of the template makes the heading the question itself, ending in a question mark, and the background starts by saying what the thing is with one concrete example before saying what changes with the answer. Written for someone who has never seen the system.

## Why
A person answers a question. A topic gives them nothing to say yes or no to. The example is there because the operator cannot picture a mechanism from its name.

## How it gets improved
Same counter as the unclear-question learning: "I don't understand" replies per round. Version stamps move when the template changes, so a chart shows which rules it was written under.
