# One message out, one reply back

## What was not good
A round took several exchanges: the questions, then the operator's answers, then research, then a re-asked question, then the challenge check on its own, then the resolution. The operator had to come back four times for one decision.

## What the updated way does
A round is one message and one reply. The message holds everything at once: the merged questions, any prototype question, and the challenge check aimed at that batch's own recommendations. Before it goes out, slot B has returned, every question is researched, and merge questions are researched too. The operator answers all of it in one reply, and the decision resolves from that reply. Operator instruction 2026-09-08: one pass over questions and answers, everything present, even if slots wait for each other. Waiting between agents is the accepted cost, a second operator pass is not. Now the rule in the skill's challenge-close section.

## Why
Each extra exchange is an operator interruption. The operator's time is the scarce resource in the chart, and the goal after the chart is zero operator actions, so the chart should already behave that way.

Count exchanges per decision in process-so-far. More than one round per decision means research or the merge was thin. Name what was missing here.
