# A batch opens with what the decision is about

## What was not good

The Peer Questions batch started at Q1. The operator had to infer from seven questions what the decision covered and asked for an introductory paragraph.

## What the updated way does

Every batch starts with one plain paragraph: what the decision is, why it exists now, and what the batch settles. Then the questions.

## Why

The operator reads one decision at a time, often hours after the last one. Questions without the ground they stand on are a puzzle first and a decision second.

## How it improves the process

One paragraph replaces the "what is this about" question that otherwise costs a round trip.
