# A ten-minute prototype for the fact everything hinges on

## What was not good
Decisions were argued from documents. The intake said herdr refuses a prompt to a busy pane "for free". Two slots and the docs agreed. The claim was wrong, and every design on top of it would have been wrong.

## What the updated way does
When one decision rests on one fact that can be measured in minutes, the chart proposes a prototype as a normal question: estimate in minutes, ten by default, a sandbox, a one-letter veto. Only the measured finding goes into the decision file. Code and scratch are discarded. Examples so far: typed into a working Codex pane and watched it drop the first task; next, link a throwaway herdr plugin to learn which pane events a hook may target.

## Why
A measurement costs ten minutes. A wrong assumption costs the decisions built on it. Under the hierarchy, a prototype is the simplest way to settle a fact, the cheapest, and it raises quality without slowing the chart.

## How it gets improved
The trigger to look for: a question whose recommended option says "the docs imply" or "should work". That sentence is the signal to propose a prototype instead of an option. Keep the operator's veto, since a prototype that runs unasked is machinery.
