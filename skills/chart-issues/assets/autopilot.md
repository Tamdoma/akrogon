# Autopilot

Read when the session's mode is autopilot or full autopilot.

While it is on, the door runs every step it runs today, and at each point where the operator would answer — fork rounds, the split proposal, the handoff review including seats — A writes the full round in its terminal, then answers it itself by the evidence, the session's signposts, and where they are silent the operator's values: simplicity, function over form, removing over adding, systems thinking, speed and price. A recommendation names the signpost that decided it. Each pick is recorded in the fork's Taken as A's, with its reason, the rejected options and any standing consultant dissent, never as operator words; this is the one exception to "Recommendations and silence cannot supply an operator answer" in [questions.md](questions.md), while approvals stay operator-only.

The first reply echoes the mode, the handed work and the consultants, and says that handed-off work waits for the operator's watcher, which the door cannot check. After the door reads the handed work, the first reply previews the session's needs sheet: every foreseen supply and approval the charting will require, each with purpose and destination. The operator's words go verbatim into each handed chart's INTAKE.md, and a mode line goes into its CHART.md with the time, the door's hc address and A's transcript path; answers the operator gave before a mid-chart switch-on stay the operator's, and a session switched on mid-chart with no signposts yet asks the signposts round before A answers anything. Under autopilot the signposts round is a presented round, not a wait: no chart parks on it and no chart work starts before it is answered. Under full autopilot A answers the signposts too; its picks are echoed in the first reply, recorded as A's in `## Autopilot` (`signposts: A`), never as operator words, and may order the handed charts but never drop one — a drop A would make is recorded only, for the operator.

These make that chart wait while the door moves on within its handed list: a pending needs-sheet row — grant approval, fixture retention and secret-save approval, a missing need — asked only with the signposts round or a changed-list proposal, never mid-chart; a new-chart proposal, an operator approval that is not a grant; a mutating or paid proof call, which waits for the operator's grant approval while other read-only proofs proceed; and a human-only prerequisite. A late find that cannot continue inside recorded authority parks the chart with one terminal statement and one `akrogon notice --attention` per park ([Take](../SKILL.md#take)), a new-chart proposal's park sends the same one notice, and each `wait:` line names the row and its status. Existing checks still stop a handoff, and A repairs what they refuse. A step on main or a lesson write that would change autopilot's own switch or limits also waits for the operator. The door never turns autopilot on, extends the handed list, drops a handed chart, changes the consultants or raises an existing bound in the skill.

A handed chart parks for a new-chart proposal only when work found while charting it is its own destination with its own observable outcome, the handed chart cannot hand off without it, and no existing open leaf or chart already covers it, judged with the research the chart already did. Work the handed chart does not need stays recorded only, a dependency serving the same outcome stays a prerequisite leaf, and splitting expressly handed intake at the map stays A's. When an existing open chart already covers the found work, A writes no proposal: the waiting chart's `wait:` line names that chart, the chart resumes once that chart's leaves are written with the same ordering as after a yes, and A takes that chart next when it is on the handed list and runnable, otherwise moving to another runnable handed chart. A parks as soon as the proposal can be stated, with its findings recorded, and takes no further fork in that chart; nothing is created for the proposed chart — no folder, seed or file — before the operator's yes, the waiting chart's Destination does not change while it waits, and consultant agreement never answers the proposal. The proposal asks, fixed in form: "chart <X> on its own (yes) or bring it into <waiting chart> (no)?" A yes is the operator's words adding <X> to the handed list, recorded as a `changed list` mode entry: A charts <X> in the same autopilot run with the usual handover, and the waiting chart resumes after <X>'s leaves are written, handing off with `blocked-by` on them when the dependent leaf shares their destination repo. A no makes A resume the waiting chart with the found work in scope as forks or prerequisite leaves, rewriting the Destination under that answer and recording the operator's no verbatim; any other operator words stay a valid answer. When the waiting chart's dependent leaf has a different destination repo than <X>'s leaves, the waiting chart stays unhanded after <X> is handed off, with one `wait:` line naming every needed <repo>/<leaf> of <X> and its landed check — each named leaf folder under that repo's `issues/closed/` — and the operator's next message in the same door resumes it; there is no cross-repo `blocked-by` and no timer. Under autopilot the handed list wins over writing one chart folder per destination.

Under autopilot every handed chart takes the lifecycle route; the direct route is not offered. Drain takes only the handed list, with no oldest-chart or seed steps, and imports nothing new. A full match between a destination seed and handed work is A's to confirm; other seed matches, open-time close offers for skipped identities, and flaky tests or failed leaves outside the handed list are recorded only. Handed work is held when another door's open chart — a chart under the registered root's `issues/chart/` whose last marker is not `Handed off`, `Closed` or `Held` and whose latest A seat in `seats.yaml` has a different hc address — shares its chart folder, GitHub identity or legacy path, or shows the same outcome in its Destination. Two doors started on the same work at the same moment is a stated limit. A failed leaf the operator hands to the door is charted like other handed work.

A consultant is dropped for the rest of the session, never re-prompted, with its completed returns kept, when it stops a second time without its return file and report ([questions.md](questions.md) `## Blind peer exchange`), when it blocks, errors or exits during an exchange, when its start fails or a send to it answers `started: false`, or when its restart fails (each seat whose restart failed is dropped with its printed result recorded under `consultant:`). A return file that arrives after the drop is ignored and recorded as ignored; a mistake in A's own call is repaired without dropping anyone. A consultant whose terminal is a consultant seat in another door's open chart is skipped the same way; the door's own charts never cause a skip.

The mode ends when every handed chart's last marker is `Handed off` or `Closed`. A chart waiting on a need, approval or prerequisite keeps the mode on, also across the end of a pass, and the operator's next message in that door — such as a supplied key — resumes it. Only the operator's words in the door's terminal turn it off early; supplying a key or changing the handed list keeps it on. Once off, an open peer turn is collected first and the next question goes to the operator.

CHART.md records the mode record and the `## Autopilot` section per [shapes.md](shapes.md), written as the pass goes. The pass ends with one line per chart in the door's terminal. The handoff footer says "handed off", not "started", and names a pause when `akrogon status` shows one. Nothing computes a score.

Under autopilot the handover fires only when switching to the next runnable handed chart, [handover.md](handover.md): old A appends `handover <time> <log>: handed <remaining list>; consultants <surviving>; signposts <path>; instructions <verbatim with provenance and scope, or none>` to the mode record of every handed chart still open, and a folder it creates for the next chart carries INTAKE.md plus CHART.md with its mode record, where an attended switch creates the CHART.md with a pending Destination; both also carry the verbatim signposts copy [handover.md](handover.md). The resume entry is `continue autopilot (handover <time>)`, after which fresh A continues the same handed list.

For a next chart whose folder old A creates for this handover, defer `seats.yaml` creation to fresh A, overriding [Open](../SKILL.md#open)'s folder-creation rule. Fresh A creates that record on entry with `opened` and its first span's `from` set to its entry time, and its first A seat uses its transcript path. Existing charts keep their original `opened` time and append the new session as usual.
